# 20000778 Evidence

Disposition: published_refutation.

Accepted proposition branch: for regular surfaces, absence of phantom subcategories is equivalent to rationality. The refuted direction is that rational surfaces have no phantom subcategories.

Primary source: Johannes Krah, "A phantom on a rational surface", arXiv:2304.01269v2; published in Inventiones mathematicae.

Read source locations:
- Abstract, page 1.
- Theorem 1.1, page 1.
- Corollary 5.2, page 6.

Evidence summary: X is the blow-up of P^2_C at 10 general points, hence rational. Theorem 1.1 constructs a non-full exceptional collection of maximal length on X, and Corollary 5.2 states that its orthogonal admissible subcategory is a universal phantom subcategory of D^b(X).

Verification: source-theorem check only; no explicit finite witness or Jacobian operation applies.
