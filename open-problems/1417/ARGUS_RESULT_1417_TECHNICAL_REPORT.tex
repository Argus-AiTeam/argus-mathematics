\title[Uniform Square-Root Edge Deficit in Critical C4,C8-Free Graphs]
{A Uniform Square-Root Edge-Deficit Bound for Strongly Critical Graphs Avoiding Four- and Eight-Cycles}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{November 4, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 05C38; Secondary 05C35, 05C07}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
Motivated by the Erd\H{o}s--Gy\'arf\'as conjecture that every graph of minimum degree at least three contains a cycle of length a power of two, we prove a parameter-uniform quadratic order bound in terms of the edge deficit $d = 2n - m$. Let $G$ be a nonempty finite simple graph of minimum degree at least three containing no simple $C_4$ or $C_8$ such that no proper subgraph of $G$ (including non-induced subgraphs) has minimum degree at least three. By combining component-sponsorship budgeting on vertices of degree at least five with an exact incidence identity that cancels all vertices of degree four, we prove that $n \le \max\{8d, 4d^2-8d\} \le 4d^2$. Consequently, any lexicographically minimal counterexample to the Erd\H{o}s--Gy\'arf\'as conjecture satisfies $m \le 2n - \max\{7, \lceil 1 + \sqrt{n+4}/2\rceil\}$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Main Mathematical Results}
\begin{theorem}[Uniform square-root edge deficit]
Let $G$ be a nonempty finite simple undirected graph with $\delta(G) \ge 3$, no simple cycle of length $4$ or $8$, and no proper subgraph $H \subsetneq G$ with $\delta(H) \ge 3$. Put $n = |V(G)|$, $m = |E(G)|$, and $d = 2n - m$. Then $d \ge 2$ and
\[
  n \;\le\; \max\{8d,\ 4d^2 - 8d\} \;\le\; 4d^2.
\]
In particular, every minimum-order, minimum-size counterexample to the Erd\H{o}s--Gy\'arf\'as conjecture satisfies $m \le 2n - \max\bigl\{7,\ \bigl\lceil 1 + \frac{\sqrt{n+4}}{2}\bigr\rceil\bigr\}$.
\end{theorem}
