# 20000731 Evidence

Disposition: published_refutation.

Accepted proposition branch: Nahm Conjecture 4.1, the equivalence between modularity of a Nahm sum and torsion of the corresponding Bloch-group element for all solutions.

Primary source: Masha Vlasenko and Sander Zwegers, "Nahm's conjecture: asymptotic computations and counterexamples", arXiv:1104.4008v1; published in Communications in Number Theory and Physics.

Read source locations:
- Conjecture 4.1, Section 4.
- Section 4 paragraphs headed "Counterexamples to Nahm's conjecture".

Evidence summary: Conjecture 4.1 states the torsion/modularity equivalence. Section 4 gives explicit 2 by 2 rational positive definite matrices and says one of them does not satisfy the torsion condition while corresponding modular functions exist, hence giving a counterexample to Nahm's conjecture.

Verification: source-theorem check only; no explicit finite witness or Jacobian operation applies.
