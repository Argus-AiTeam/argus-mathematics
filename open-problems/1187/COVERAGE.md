# Six-copy split and centroid coverage

The subgroup is literally `H=<XyyxYYY,XyxxyXX>` in the fixed ambient
`F(x,y)`. Capitals denote inverses. The accepted bound is `w_prim(H)>=6`;
neither the historical negative construction runs nor this reduction alone
implies equality or a stronger lower bound.

**Theorem (computer-assisted; independent Reviewer acceptance pending).**
For every `1<=q<=6` and every `c_1,...,c_q in F(x,y)`, the subgroup

    <c_1 H c_1^-1, ..., c_q H c_q^-1>

contains no ambient primitive. Consequently `w_prim(H)>=7`, with infinity
allowed. There is no bound on carrier lengths or on primitive-word lengths.
This strengthens the strongest accepted bound, `w_prim(H)>=6`; it does not
give exact width seven, a stronger AC-layer bound, or an AC counterexample.
The proof is the structural reduction below together with the completed
exact certificate replay at the end.

## Structural reduction

Apply the accepted all-carrier colored-component lemma
(`marked-cancellation/CONJUGATE_WIDTH.md`, Section 1). A primitive in a join
of at most six arbitrary conjugates has a conjugate carried by a component
obtained by vertex-attaching at most six original eleven-vertex cores along
a tree, followed by the least Stallings folding. A component using at most
five original copies is excluded by the independently accepted five-copy
theorem (`p2-five-conjugate-sharpness/PUBLICATION.md`, Theorem 1).
Thus only six-copy trees need a new decision.

Every tree on six vertices has either an edge with two three-vertex sides,
or a unique centroid whose branch sizes are `2+2+1`, `2+1+1+1`, or
`1+1+1+1+1`. Indeed, descend into a component containing more than half of
the vertices until none does. At the resulting vertex every branch has
size at most three. A size-three branch gives the first alternative.
Otherwise all branch sizes are at most two and sum to five, giving exactly
the three displayed partitions. No assumption about attachment phases,
carrier lengths, or primitive-word lengths is used.

## The balanced edge

Fold each three-copy side before making the remaining attachment.
Least folding commutes with making these identifications: both procedures
generate the same least equivalence relation containing the tree attachments
and closed under equal-labelled outgoing edges. Enumerate every vertex pair
between the two folded sides.

An unbased labelled isomorphism of a half may change its basepoint; it is
not equality of the original based subgroups. The catalogue therefore
retains an original-copy attachment tree and the image of every original
phase in the canonical graph. Every canonical vertex has such a preimage.
For attachment vertices `a,b`, choose those preimages, reroot the second
copy-tree at its selected copy, and join the two original trees there.
This recovers six literal conjugating words, not just endpoint maps.

The half catalogue itself is exhaustive by leaf deletion. There is one
one-copy type. All pairs of its vertices with original H vertices give all
two-copy types. Every three-copy tree has a leaf; delete it, normalize the
two-copy remainder, and attach at every vertex of that remainder and every
phase of the leaf. The retained original-phase isomorphisms lift these
attachments to actual original-copy trees. All vertices are covered by
original cores, as in the accepted colored-component lemma.

Canonical half types already obtainable from at most two original copies
can be omitted: after changing the basepoint along a labelled path, the
same based half subgroup is generated by at most two actual H conjugates.
Joining the other half then uses at most five. An isomorphism of the whole
folded core, with the attaching vertex retained, supplies this based
equality. Equal partial maps of individual carriers would not suffice.
Explicitly, if the alternative half is based at `o`, its carriers are `d_i`,
and `t` labels a path from `o` to the attaching vertex, its subgroup based
at that vertex is generated by the actual carriers `t^-1 d_i`.
This is the replacement used inside the join, rather than the unshifted
`d_i`. The other half is based at the common attaching vertex as well.
The unordered half pairs are sufficient because exchanging the two sides
does not change the unbased joined graph; all vertex pairs are still used.

## The centroid

Keep the central H basepoint fixed. Write `q_v` for a path from that base
to original phase `v`. A leaf at phases `(a,b)` contributes the based join

    J(a,b) = <H, q_a q_b^-1 H q_b q_a^-1>.

A two-copy branch at phases `(a,b,c,d)` contributes the based join

    K(a,b,c,d) = <H, u H u^-1, v H v^-1>,
    u = q_a q_b^-1,  v = u q_c q_d^-1.

All 121 leaf coordinates and all 14641 branch coordinates are normalized
by **based labelled graph equality**. The central H is a subgroup of every
one of these joins. Replacing a branch by an equal based join therefore
preserves its join with every other branch, including all interactions
created by subsequent folding.

A leaf equal to H, a repeated leaf type, or a two-copy branch equal to H
or a leaf join saves at least one original copy. Two equal two-copy branch
types also save copies. Such outcomes are covered by the five-copy theorem,
not inferred to be negative from a failed search. Permuting distinct
branches does not change their based join. Consequently it suffices to
enumerate five distinct nontrivial leaf types, one genuine two-copy branch
with three distinct nontrivial leaf types, and two distinct genuine
two-copy branches with one nontrivial leaf type. No other omission is made.

## Exact decisions and replay

For a fixed attachment assignment, the desired vertex equivalence is the
least equivalence containing its tree identifications and satisfying:
equivalent sources of equally labelled edges have equivalent targets.
The queue engine initially identifies only the requested attaching vertices.
Each subsequent union is forced by this implication. At termination each
class has at most one outgoing edge of each label, so no additional union is
necessary. This proves leastness in both directions. The reference engine
instead retains the original edge list and repeatedly scans this implication
until there is no change; it computes the same least equivalence without
the mutable outgoing-edge merge table.

Rooted canonicalization traverses all four labels in the fixed order
`x,X,y,Y` from the fixed basepoint. Unbased canonicalization minimizes those
complete traversals over all vertices. Equal codes therefore give a labelled
graph isomorphism, based in the first case. No ambient basis automorphism is
applied. Retained phase maps are checked as bijections preserving every
labelled edge and every missing edge, not just as path endpoint assignments.

The source `coverage.py` constructs the normalization maps and retains the
literal reconstruction data. `cover.cpp` enumerates indexed domains,
performing genuine least folds. Its output retains every domain unit and
every coherent attachment together with the actual folded labelled graph.
An omitted attachment must have all four signed directed quadrants acyclic;
this is an exact sufficient condition for primitive exclusion.

Every retained coherent graph must be bound to a complete negative
signed-positive-Nielsen language, or to an explicit positive certificate.
A negative language starts with the two signed generator partial maps for
each of the four sign choices. It contains its initial pair, is closed under
`(f,g) -> (g o f,g)` and `(f,g) -> (f,g o f)`, and has no fixed point in
either coordinate of any retained pair. These are precisely the hypotheses
of the accepted finite primitive-language theorem
(`p2-five-conjugate-sharpness/PUBLICATION.md`, lines 120-168).
Every vertex is tested; unbased canonicalization thus preserves the
decision. `verify_negative` checks the initial maps, both transitions and
absence of fixed points against the exact graph, rather than trusting a
reported negative outcome or a state total.
A positive result must reconstruct all six original copies and pass both
`verify_joint.py` and `verify_six.py`, including literal membership and both
inverse ambient substitutions. A negative conclusion requires all indexed
units, the complete normalization maps, all graph-language bindings, and
replay of the omitted acyclic cases. Aggregate counts alone are insufficient.

The normalization gives 1,001 half types through three copies, of which
975 require three in this catalogue, 50 nontrivial based leaf types, and
2,075 based branch types not equal to H or a leaf join. Its full domains are:

A split unit is one unordered half-type pair with all its vertex pairs.
The three centroid unit conventions fix the first choice and enumerate the
remaining choices lexicographically. These are counts of indexed inputs,
not of distinct resulting subgroups. The split count also equals
`((sum n_i)^2 + sum n_i^2)/2`, with `sum n_i=27935` and
`sum n_i^2=803025`, independently of their enumeration order.

All these indexed domains and bindings have now passed the reference-engine
replay, not merely a comparison of totals. Publication originality and
significance remain separate independent judgements.

Historical star and one-branch runs supply the normalization identities and
domain sizes, but do not retain all indexed graph-language bindings. Their
aggregate outcomes cannot be reused as those missing certificates. The new
execution therefore records those bindings rather than assuming them.
The accepted five-copy theorem and primitive-language decision theorem are
reused without re-enumerating their accepted certificates.

The Jacobian free-group capability finding in `joint-synthesis` has no matching
Stallings or nonabelian primitive operation. The inspected and executed
`graph.tree_decomposition.reroot.compute` control here checks only rerooting a
three-node copy tree. Its exact payload, typed output, server version,
protocol and adapter status are retained in dedicated JSON files. It supplies
no free-group exclusion or primitive certificate.

## Completed exact replay and conclusion

`verification.json` records a successful full replay at campaign root
`/home/argustest/zimo/counterexample-batch-1/campaigns/1187`.
The separate edge-list fold engine recomputed every indexed choice,
including every omitted sign-acyclic case. Every coherent record matched
its actual least-fold graph and its negative-language identifier.
All four reference processes exited zero. The same run checked all 20,449
normalization indices, all 1,001 half reconstruction models, and all 9,941
retained canonical negative-language graphs, comprising 2,085,750 states.
The exact indexed bindings are the four `*.cover.gz` files; the complete
languages are `languages.jsonl.gz`. These are certificates for the retained
domains, not a claim that 9,941 is the number of all six-copy subgroup types.

Suppose a join in the theorem contained a primitive. By the accepted
colored-component lemma, a cyclic conjugate is carried by a vertex-attached
tree component using at most six original copies. At most five copies are
excluded by the accepted five-copy theorem. A six-copy tree has one of the
four shapes above. Every omitted normalized type has an actual at-most-five
conjugate representation. Every retained type occurs in the verified indexed
domain. A sign-acyclic output cannot contain the primitive; a coherent output
cannot contain it by its checked complete negative language. This is a
contradiction. Thus the theorem holds, and its width corollary follows from
the definition of `w_prim(H)`.

The computation is finite, but its conclusion is not a bounded-word search
boundary: the proved structural reduction covers arbitrary original
conjugators and the complete primitive language has no word cutoff.
