# 20000472 Evidence

Disposition: published_refutation.

Accepted proposition: in the Bargmann-Fock setting, for measurable symbols g with g k_a in L^2(dmu) for every normalized reproducing kernel k_a, boundedness of T_g is equivalent to boundedness of the heat transform g^(1/4).

Primary source: Sam Looi, "A counterexample to the Berger-Coburn conjecture", arXiv:2601.20859v1.

Read source locations:
- Abstract, page 1.
- Conjecture 1.1, page 2.
- Theorem 1.2, page 3.
- Theorem 6.5 and following proof, Section 6.

Evidence summary: Conjecture 1.1 states the Berger-Coburn endpoint criterion under the measurable-symbol and kernel-domain hypotheses. Theorem 1.2 constructs a measurable symbol g such that the Toeplitz operator extends to a bounded operator while g^(1/4) is unbounded on C^n. Section 6 supplies the construction at t=1/4.

Jacobian applicability: source-theorem check only. A catalog search of the installed Jacobian operations found no Toeplitz, Fock, Bargmann-Fock, heat-transform, or Bargmann operation; the only Weyl match was a finite root-system Weyl-group-order operation, unrelated to this analytic Toeplitz statement.

Review decision: accept this package as a published_refutation. The row accurately records a primary-source refutation of the endpoint iff criterion for general measurable symbols. The limitation wording is honest because it does not claim anything about structured symbol subclasses or the forward implication separately.
