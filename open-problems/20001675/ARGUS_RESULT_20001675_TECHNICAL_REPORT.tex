\title[Exponential HOMFLY-PT Fibers of Plabic Links and Knots]
{Arbitrary-Depth Exponential HOMFLY-PT Fibers over Quiver Point-Counting Polynomials of Plabic Links and Knots}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{November 4, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 57K14; Secondary 05C31, 13F60}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
In connection with the Galashin--Lam problem on whether the recursive $\mathbb{F}_q$ quiver point-counting polynomial of a simple plabic graph determines the full two-variable HOMFLY-PT polynomial of its associated plabic link, we construct explicit infinite families of connected, simple, non-reduced plabic realizations of trees and corona-lifted trees of bounded degree. For every integer jet depth $r \ge 1$ and word length $m \ge 1$, we obtain $2^m$ connected simple plabic realizations on $N = 2n(r)m+8$ vertices ($n(r) \le 37r^2$) whose associated links are single-component knots sharing the same normalized quiver point-counting polynomial, the same Alexander specialization, the same first $r$ top-$a$ HOMFLY-PT layers, and all $a$-derivatives of order less than $r$ at $a=1$, while producing $2^m$ pairwise distinct two-variable HOMFLY-PT polynomials ($\log_2|\text{fiber}| \ge (N-8)/(74r^2)$).
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section{Main Mathematical Results}
\begin{theorem}[Arbitrary-depth exponential HOMFLY-PT fibers for plabic knots]
For every pair of integers $r,m \ge 1$, there exists an explicit family of $2^m$ connected trees $T^+_\epsilon$ ($\epsilon \in \{0,1\}^m$) of maximum degree at most six on $N = 2n(r)m+8$ vertices ($n(r) \le 37r^2$) whose polygon-cell plabic disk realizations are connected, simple, non-reduced, and yield single-component knots such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
  \item All $2^m$ members have identical normalized quiver point-counting polynomial $R(q)$, identical Alexander specialization, and identical HOMFLY-PT derivatives of all orders $< r$ at $a=1$;
  \item Their $r$-th derivatives at $a=1$, and hence their full two-variable HOMFLY-PT polynomials $P(a,z)$, are pairwise distinct, giving $\log_2|\text{fiber}| \ge (N-8)/(74r^2)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
