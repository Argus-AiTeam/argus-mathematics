\title[Threshold Discrepancy Barriers for Depth-Three Planted-Clique AMP Schedules]
{Threshold Discrepancy Barriers for Depth-Three Scalar Planted-Clique AMP Schedules}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}

\subjclass[2020]{Primary 60B20; Secondary 68Q87, 62H30}

\begin{abstract}
For the planted-clique recovery problem at clique size $k=\lceil\sqrt{n}/\log n\rceil$, we prove two exact limitations on frozen scalar approximate message passing (AMP) / cavity transfer schedules: (1)~uniform positive-semidefinite domination via the collision Gram matrix alone fails at reachable parity states; and (2)~every admissible rooted-$C_4$, depth-three scalar score has root-in/root-out threshold discrepancy $O(1/\log n + n^{-1/2})$, preventing sub-$\sqrt{n}$ candidate isolation.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Main Barriers}
\begin{theorem}
At $k=\lceil\sqrt{n}/\log n\rceil$ in $G(n,1/2,k)$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
  \item Under admissible Lipschitz scalar denoisers, a reachable parity configuration sends a zero root-out shifted-Gram block to a nonzero rank-one all-ones block, ruling out uniform PSD pullback domination by the collision Gram matrix.
  \item For the rooted-$C_4$ depth-three scalar message schedule, signed diagonal cancellation incurs a loss of $k-1$ and bounds the root-in versus root-out score discrepancy by $O(1/\log n + n^{-1/2}) = o(1)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
