\title[Weighted Dijoin Packing in One-Fill Chordal and Single-Defect Digraphs]
{Weighted Dijoin Packing in One-Edge Deletions of Chordal Digraphs and Six-Terminal Single-Defect Digraphs}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{November 4, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 05C20; Secondary 90C27, 05C70}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We establish two unconditional extensions of Woodall's 1976 dijoin packing conjecture $\nu(D,w) = \tau(D,w)$ for nonnegative integer arc capacities $w \colon A \to \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$. First, extending the 2025 theorem of Cornu\'ejols, Liu, and Ravi for weighted chordal digraphs, we prove that $\nu(D,w) = \tau(D,w)$ holds for every finite loopless digraph $D$ whose underlying simple undirected graph becomes chordal after adding at most one edge. Second, in the single source-sink reachability defect setting (where the condensation has at most one unreachable ordered source-sink pair), we prove weighted dijoin packing for all topologies with at most six terminals and establish that any globally minimal missing-color configuration in an arbitrary-size single-defect digraph must have Boolean quotient rank at least five (surpassing the rank-four boundary of Williams's 2004 conjecture).
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section{Main Mathematical Results}
\begin{theorem}[Weighted dijoin packing for one-fill chordal and six-terminal single-defect digraphs]
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
  \item For every finite loopless digraph $D=(V,A)$ whose underlying simple graph is chordal or becomes chordal upon adding one edge, and every $w \colon A \to \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ with $\tau(D,w) < \infty$, $\nu(D,w) = \tau(D,w)$.
  \item For every finite directed multigraph $D$ with $w \colon A \to \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ whose condensation has at most one unreachable source-sink pair and at most six terminal vertices, $\nu(D,w) = \tau(D,w)$; furthermore, any minimal missing-color configuration in a general single-defect digraph has Boolean quotient rank at least $5$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
