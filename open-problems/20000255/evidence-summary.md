# 20000255 Evidence

Disposition: published_refutation.

Accepted proposition branch: the claim that terminal or log terminal singularities are necessarily Cohen-Macaulay. The separate question of rational singularities is not certified by this row.

Primary source: Burt Totaro, "Terminal 3-folds that are not Cohen-Macaulay", arXiv:2407.02608v2.

Read source locations:
- Abstract and introduction, page 1.
- Theorem 0.1, page 1.
- Cited earlier characteristic-two example, page 1, referencing Theorem 5.1 of Totaro's earlier paper.

Evidence summary: the source states that there are terminal singularities of dimension 3 that are not Cohen-Macaulay, and Theorem 0.1 gives a mixed-characteristic quotient Y/G that is terminal, not Cohen-Macaulay, three-dimensional, and flat over Z_2.

Verification: source-theorem check only; no explicit finite witness or Jacobian operation applies.
