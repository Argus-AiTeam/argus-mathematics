# 20000496 Evidence

Disposition: published_refutation.

Accepted proposition branch: for convex domains in R^n, n>=3, a first nontrivial Neumann eigenfunction has no global extremum in the interior.

Primary source: Jaume de Dios Pont, "Convex sets can have interior hot spots", arXiv:2412.06344v1.

Read source locations:
- Abstract, page 1.
- Definition 1.1 and Theorem 1.2, pages 1-2.

Evidence summary: the paper states the hot spots conjecture for convex bounded domains and constructs counterexamples for all sufficiently large dimensions. Theorem 1.2 gives smooth centrally symmetric convex domains with spectral gap for which the eigenfunction maximum ratio exceeds the boundary maximum, so for large dimension the maximum is attained only in the interior.

Verification: source-theorem check only; no explicit finite witness or Jacobian operation applies.
