\title[Centered Nuclear Duality and Pinning in Monotone Circuit Complexity]
{Centered Nuclear Duality, Addition-Aware Pinning, and Relative-Mass Obstructions in Monotone Boolean Circuit Lower Bounds}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{November 4, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 68Q17; Secondary 94C10, 06E30}

\begin{abstract}
In the study of monotone and positive-weight Boolean circuit lower bounds for fixed-factor approximation of $R_n = 2F_G - T_G$ over the full Boolean cube, we establish a two-sided equivalence between constant-factor circuit approximation bounds and a centered nuclear-error dual charge, prove an addition-aware pinning decomposition and fixed-tolerance signed-test transfer reduction, and construct explicit $O(M_n+N^2)$-gate nonnegative rational circuits and affine-line incidence matrices over $\mathbb{F}_{4^\ell}$ refuting uncentered relative-mass, naive rarity-budget, and sampler-free matrix-gluing shortcuts.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section{Main Mathematical Results}
\begin{theorem}[Centered nuclear-error duality, addition-aware pinning, and shortcut refutations]
Approximating $R_n = 2F_G - T_G$ within a fixed factor $\kappa_0 > 1$ on the Boolean cube under the canonical sampler is equivalent up to absolute constants to bounding the centered nuclear-error dual charge and reduces via addition-aware pinning to the outer-sampler signed test, whereas uncentered relative-mass and sampler-free matrix-gluing relaxations are refuted by explicit $O(M_n+N^2)$-gate and $\mathbb{F}_{4^\ell}$ affine-plane constructions.
\end{theorem}
