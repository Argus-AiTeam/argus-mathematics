\paragraph{Result and unresolved obligation.}
This is an upper construction, not another discrepancy lower bound.
The reviewed HC construction supplies a full-domain positive cover.
Its entropy can be bounded by retained-mass-weighted box surprisal,
plus the logarithm of the original cut count and a bounded term.

CDR18, MP9, explicit native constants, a positive fixed defect

\section{The construction and its single loss}
Let $Q=\sum_{j=1}^r A_jV_j$, $1\le r\le s$, be the nonzero
terms of the actual cut of the original size-$s$ DAG. Its source
partitions $S_j,\overline S_j$ are balanced. Put
$h_j(\nu)=(A_jV_j)(X_\nu)$, $h=\sum_jh_j$, and
$H=\max_{\Om}h\le2\kappa$.

For a finite family of source boxes $U$, use the actual HC4
completion $R_{j,U}$ and error $E_{j,U}=R_{j,U}-h_j$ on $\Om(U)$.
Choose
\[
 \max_{\Om(U)}E_{j,U}\le\delta_{j,U}\le D_j(U),\qquad
 T_{j,U}=1_{\Om(U)}(R_{j,U}-\delta_{j,U})_+ .       \tag{HE1}
\]
Every $T_{j,U}$ is an exact nonnegative rectangle sum across
$S_j,\overline S_j$, with total coefficient weight
$m_{j,U}=\max T_{j,U}\le H$. Its rectangles are subsets of $U$.
On the entire original domain $0\le T_{j,U}\le h_j$.

Fix nonnegative $\lambda_U$ and $0\le\rho_{j,U}\le1$ before
drawing the original sampler, subject to
\[
 c(\nu)=\sum_U\lambda_U1_{\Om(U)}(\nu)\le1
                         \quad(\nu\in\Om).
 \quad
 H_U=\sum_j\rho_{j,U}T_{j,U},\qquad
 P=\sum_U\lambda_UH_U.                              \tag{HE2}
\]
Thus $P\le ch\le h$ even at zero-probability assignments.
With $p=\Delta$ the unchanged complete law, set
\[
 \begin{split}
 p_U&=p(\Om(U)),& Z_U&=\E_pH_U,&
 W_U&=\sum_j\rho_{j,U}m_{j,U}\le rH,\\
 \alpha&=\sum_U\lambda_Up_U\le1,&
 Z&=\sum_U\lambda_UZ_U=\E_pP .
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{HE3}
\]
The exact loss, with no separate per-cut allowance, is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \Theta={}&\E_p[(1-c)h]\\
 &+\sum_U\lambda_U\E_p\left[1_{\Om(U)}
   \sum_j\left((1-\rho_{j,U})h_j+
     \rho_{j,U}\min\{h_j,\delta_{j,U}-E_{j,U}\}\right)\right]\\
 ={}&\E_p(h-P).                                      \tag{HE4}
 \end{split}
\]
This is HC13a, without replacing an actual lost mass by its error
allowance. At each box $\sum_jD_j(U)=D_Q(U)$ remains the joint
original charge. No separately maximized defects are added.

\section{Cost the rectangles before discarding their probabilities}
For any probability law $p$ on the finite original domain, write
$c_p(B)=p(B)\log(1/p(B))$, with zero value at $p(B)=0$.
If $f=\sum_i a_i1_{B_i}$, $a_i\ge0$, let $W=\sum_i a_i$
and $z=\E_pf$. Concavity of $x\log(1/x)$ gives
\[
 \sum_i a_i c_p(B_i)\le z\log(W/z)\quad(z>0).          \tag{HE5}
\]
Indeed, apply concavity with weights $a_i/W$ to the probabilities
$p(B_i)$, whose weighted mean is $z/W$. The function is continuous
at zero, so zero-law rectangles are included. When $z=0$, both
entropy cost and its continuously interpreted upper bound are zero.

\begin{proposition}
Suppose the construction (HE1)--(HE3) has $Z>0$. There is an
explicit full-domain representation of $P$ by balanced original
rectangles, of entropy cost $C_p(w)$, satisfying
\[
 \begin{split}
 C_p(w)&\le
    \sum_{U:Z_U>0}\lambda_UZ_U\log(W_U/Z_U),           \tag{HE6}\\
 \frac{C_p(w)}Z&\le
     J+\log(rH\alpha/Z),\\
 J&=\sum_{U:Z_U>0}\frac{\lambda_UZ_U}{Z}
                              \log(1/p_U).           \tag{HE7}
 \end{split}
\]
All boxes, factors, and probabilities are evaluated under the
same original law. The weights in $J$ are retained means, not
uniform box weights, raw $\lambda_U$, or conditioned-history weights.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Expand each hinge by HC3, multiply its weights by
$\lambda_U\rho_{j,U}$, and add. Each rectangle keeps its original
partition; intersecting its two sides with $U$ preserves that
partition. No signed correction is made. Apply (HE5) to the
combined hinge representation of $H_U$, whose total coefficient
weight is exactly $W_U$. This proves (HE6).

For $p_U>0$ put $a_U=Z_U/p_U$, allowing $a_U=0$.
Since $W_U\le rH$, division by $Z$ gives
\[
 \frac{C_p(w)}Z\le J+\log(rH)
               -\frac1Z\sum_{U:p_U>0}
                                  \lambda_Up_Ua_U\log a_U.
                                                               \tag{HE8}
\]
The weights $\lambda_Up_U/\alpha$ form a probability vector,
and their mean $a_U$ is $Z/\alpha$. Convexity of $x\log x$,
with $0\log0=0$, therefore gives
\[
 \sum_U\lambda_Up_Ua_U\log a_U\ge Z\log(Z/\alpha).
                                                               \tag{HE9}
\]
Since $Z>0$ implies $\alpha>0$, substitution proves (HE7).
If $p_U=0$, then $Z_U=0$; its entropy contribution is zero but
its full-domain rectangles and packing constraints were not removed.
\end{proof}

In particular, under BP1 and the single condition $\Theta\le1/16$,
\[
 Z\ge1/\kappa-1/16,\qquad
 \frac{rH\alpha}{Z}\le
     r\,\frac{2\kappa^2}{1-\kappa/16}
 \le r\,\frac{4225}{1918}<\frac83\,r .
                                                               \tag{HE10}
\]
The exponential series gives $e>1+1+1/2+1/6=8/3$. Consequently
\[
                  C_p(w)/Z<J+\log(s+1)+1.             \tag{HE11}
\]
No LR3 normalization is used here: $r$ counts the nonzero original
cut terms, not gates of a materialized normalized circuit.
If such a circuit were recut instead, the existing LR3 overhead
would have to be charged. Real coefficients, sharing, repeated
powers, and unrestricted degree remain covered by K2 and HC3.

\section{Precisely what would imply the all-multiplier estimate}
Retain the full size-dependent proposed budget
\[
 T=K_0+a\log(M_n+2)+\beta\log(s+1)+N/2^{139}.          \tag{HE12}
\]
If for every native input circuit an original-law packing could
be constructed with
\[
              \Theta\le1/16,\qquad
              J\le T-\log(s+1)-1,                    \tag{HE13}
\]
then the same $P$ would satisfy the MP9 domination and entropy
requirements. For every $\eta\ge0$, its soft-retention objective
would obey, without choosing a multiplier first,
\[
 \begin{split}
 {\cal E}_{p,T+\eta}(h)
 &\le C_p(w)+(T+\eta)\Theta\\
 &\le TZ+(T+\eta)\Theta
   =T\E_ph+\eta\Theta
   \le T\E_ph+\eta/16.                               \tag{HE14}
 \end{split}
\]
This proves CDR18 \emph{conditional on (HE13)}, not unconditionally.
It is a sufficient construction criterion, not a new equivalence
and not a necessary restriction on globally pooled MP9 covers.

The missing step is the simultaneous construction in (HE13) using
the original circuit size and a fixed positive defect threshold.
The packing inequality alone does not bound $J$ at the required
rate. Conversely the old scalar lower bound on
$\sum_U\lambda_U$ is not a lower bound on (HE6), on $J$, or on an
optimally pooled cover. No repeated scalar-volume test is used
here. No values of $K_0,a,\beta,b_*,N_0$ satisfying the native
quantifiers have been established, so primal attainment and
GL10--GL13 cannot yet be invoked to announce a fixed-factor theorem.

\section{Exact finite upper-construction replay}
This is a finite diagnostic under a declared test law, not the
complete production sampler. Four binary sources are denoted
$x,y,z,w$; all factors below use cubes of their literal variables.
On Boolean inputs those cubes have the literal values. With
$t=1/256$, share the interaction $I=t x_1^3z_1^3$ and set
\[
 A=(x_0^3+x_1^3+I)(y_0^3+y_1^3),\quad
 V=(z_0^3+z_1^3+I)(w_0^3+w_1^3),\quad Q=AV.          \tag{HE15}
\]
Build $V$ before $A$. The nonzero actual cut is $A$ with context
$V$ and original partition $\{x,y\}\mid\{z,w\}$.
The replay checks this using the existing divided-difference cut,
not an invented factor label. All $256$ Boolean inputs satisfy
$F\le Q\le(1+t)^2F$, and exactly $b_Q=t/2$.
No small production-$R_n$ circuit is asserted.

The test law fixes $y=w=0$ and makes $x,z$ independent fair bits.
Use boxes $\Om,\{x=0\},\{x=1\},\{y=0\},\{y=1\}$ with weights
$1/4,1/4,1/4,1/2,1/2$, respectively. They pack with $c=1$ on
all $16$ assignments, including the $12$ zero-law assignments.
Take the actual maximum completion error as each allowance and
retain every hinge. Direct exact calculation gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \Theta&=7t/16<1/16,&
 Z&=1+t/16+t^2/4,\\
 C_p(w)/\log2&=1/4+7t/8+t^2/2,&
 ZJ/\log2&=1/4+(t+t^2)/16,\\
 \sum_U\lambda_UW_U&=7/4+3t/2+3t^2/2.                \tag{HE16}
 \end{split}
\]
Here $H=(1+t)^2$, $r=\alpha=1$. The upper bound
$J+\log(H/Z)$ is strictly smaller than the older global-weight
bound $\log((\sum_U\lambda_UW_U)/Z)$. The replay proves this
without floating point: $J/\log2<1/3$ and
$1-H/(\sum_U\lambda_UW_U)>1/3$, using
$\log2<1$ and $\log u\ge1-1/u$ for $u\ge1$.
It also verifies the entropy upper inequality using the same
rational logarithm bounds. A second case simultaneously reduces
packing coverage and one hinge's retained fraction, checking
the uncovered and partial-retention terms of (HE4).

One bounded Jacobian mutual-information operation checks only
the constant-hinge specialization of this packing. Its exact
joint law is $p(\nu)\lambda_U1_U(\nu)$, its information is
$1/4$ bits, and the zero-law box is retained as a zero column.
The first request with all sixteen rows exceeded the operation's
bounded cell count and was rejected before computation. Its exact
payload and validation error are retained. The corrected request
omits only zero-probability rows of this auxiliary joint table,
leaving twenty cells; the local cover replay still checks all
sixteen domain points.
It does not certify the circuit theorem or supply native constants.
The source \texttt{hinge\_entropy\_upper\_check.py} independently
replays (HE15)--(HE16), all full-domain constraints, and the
retained typed adapter certificate. The general upper proof
is (HE5)--(HE9), not the finite replay.
