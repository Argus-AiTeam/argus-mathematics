\title[Conformal Transfer for Euler Flows in Curved Pipes]
{Conformal Transfer and Curvature-Response Closure for Axisymmetric Euler Flows in Periodic Variable-Radius Pipes}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{November 4, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 35Q31; Secondary 76B03, 35B44}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
In connection with the regularity and finite-time singularity problem for the three-dimensional incompressible Euler equations, we investigate the transfer of the Chen--Hou axisymmetric boundary blowup framework from a straight periodic cylinder to periodic variable-radius Euclidean pipes $P_\kappa = \{(R,\vartheta,Z) : 0 \le R < 1+\kappa(1-\cos Z),\ Z \in \mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}\}$. We first construct an explicit family of stream functions with vanishing origin jets that refutes the direct Cartesian weighted Biot--Savart inverse estimate via a logarithmic divergence. We then introduce an adapted conformal coordinate chart that eliminates the anisotropic principal part and prove closed weighted inverse, transport, and curvature-response bounds uniformly for $0 < |\kappa| \le 1/10$.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section{Main Mathematical Results}
\begin{theorem}[Cartesian logarithmic obstruction and conformal response closure]
On the periodic variable-radius pipe $P_\kappa$ ($0 < |\kappa| \le 1/10$):
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
  \item The uncorrected Cartesian weighted inverse operator exhibits a linear-in-mode amplification on zero-flux stream functions with vanishing origin jets, refuting the naive cylindrical transfer inequality;
  \item In the adapted conformal chart $(\rho,\eta)$, the transformed stream-vorticity inverse, singular weight transport, and curvature-coupling remainder satisfy closed quantitative energy and holder-weighted bounds across all separate angular modes.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
