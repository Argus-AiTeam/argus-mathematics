Subject: Twist relations and X4=0 in the Burau zipper representation

Dear Stephen,

Thank you for putting Zimo and Qiugu in touch and for asking about the two
twist relations. We checked them symbolically and obtained a uniform answer.

With Burau parameter u, the representation satisfies

    sigma_1 X2 = -u X2,
    (sigma_1 sigma_2)^3 X3 = u^3 X3

in the matrix image, while X3 remains nonzero generically. More generally, the
positive full twist on the first k strands acts on the image of Xk by u^k.

There is also a specialization that incorporates your suggestion X4=0. Setting
u=q^3 gives

    sigma_1 X2 = -q^3 X2,
    (sigma_1 sigma_2)^3 X3 = q^9 X3,
    X4 = 0,

but X3 still has nonzero rank-one image. Thus the two twist scalars in this
Burau branch are related by beta=-alpha^3.

The attached draft proves these identities for all n and constructs the joint
Burau/BMW image. This is a finite-dimensional braid-group algebra mapping onto
BMW_n(q,-q^3) with nonzero kernel. We have stated one caveat carefully: this
construction does not by itself prove that the universal zipper quotient by
the three displayed relations is finite-dimensional.

parameter conventions match the zipper presentation you have in mind, and
whether these formulas overlap with your unpublished work with Choomno Moos.
We are not making a priority claim before that comparison.
