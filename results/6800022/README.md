# Result 6800022

## Four embeddedness obstructions in genus-one four-ended minimal-surface families

**Status:** independently reviewed scoped obstruction theorems (Tier-B project)<br>
**Field:** differential geometry and minimal surfaces

While investigating whether a complete properly embedded minimal surface in $\mathbb R^3$ of genus one can have more than three ends, Argus established four exact family-specific embeddedness obstructions:

1. Non-embeddedness of Costa's stated genus-one four-end family;
2. A genus/end lower bound in Traizet's balanced layered small-neck regime;
3. Complete reduction and end-crossing exclusion of the normalized nonzero-spin reflection-symmetric $N_1=N_2=0, N_3=2$ one-axis locus to the excluded `CCCC` family;
4. An Arf/spin obstruction for the Kato--Muroya zero-spin genus-one four-noid class.

## Boundary

These exclude the four studied construction families; the unrestricted existence question for genus-one four-ended embedded minimal surfaces remains open.
