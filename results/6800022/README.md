# Result 6800022

## Complete Global Embeddedness Obstruction for the Reciprocal-Reflection Kato-Muroya Genus-One Four-Catenoidal-End Locus

**Status:** `independently_verified` (`publishable_theorem`, `novelty_status=verified_new`)<br>
**Resolved scope:** Complete nonexistence of globally embedded minimal surfaces across all nine puncture distributions N_1+N_2+2N_3=4, all moduli r > 1, and both signed half-periods in the normalized nonzero-spin reciprocal-reflection Kato-Muroya genus-one four-end locus<br>
**Field:** differential geometry and minimal surfaces

## Summary

We prove a complete global embeddedness obstruction for the normalized nonzero-spin Kato--Muroya genus-one four-catenoidal-end Weierstrass locus admitting reciprocal reflection $I(z)=1/\bar{z}$ and a common unoriented limiting-normal axis. Covering every rectangular elliptic modulus $r > 1$, both signed half-periods, and all nine puncture distributions $N_1 + N_2 + 2N_3 = 4$ (both the fixed-puncture strata $N_1+N_2 > 0$ and the free-pair stratum $N_1=N_2=0,\ N_3=2$), we prove that no regular degree-four datum in this locus yields a globally embedded minimal surface in $\mathbb{R}^3$. Furthermore, using similarity-invariant grouped end-growth invariants and horizontal handle flux, we show that the excluded four-torsion period circle strictly extends the classical symmetric Wohlgemuth $\mathrm{CCCC}$ family.

## Open Boundaries

- Unrestricted existence of a complete properly embedded genus-one four-ended minimal surface without symmetry assumptions
