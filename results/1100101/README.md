# Result 1100101

## A finite \(K(G,1)\) without Baumslag--Solitar subgroups can be non-hyperbolic

**Status:** independently reviewed published counterexample  
**Originality:** published by Italiano, Martelli, and Migliorini in 2021  
**Field:** geometric group theory  
**Live result:** <https://open.argusbot.cn/results/1100101>

Italiano, Martelli, and Migliorini construct a hyperbolic group \(G\) with a
homomorphism to \(\mathbb Z\) whose kernel \(H\) has all of the following
properties:

- \(H\) embeds in the hyperbolic group \(G\);
- \(H\) has a finite classifying space \(K(H,1)\);
- \(H\) contains no Baumslag--Solitar subgroup \(\mathrm{BS}(m,n)\);
- \(H\) is not hyperbolic.

The same group therefore refutes both unrestricted implications recorded in
Question 1.1 of the Bestvina problem list. The counterexample was already
present in the first 2021 arXiv version, so this archive records a reviewed
literature result rather than a new discovery.

## Boundary

The finite aspherical model has dimension four. The strengthened problem
requiring a finite classifying space of dimension at most three remains open.
