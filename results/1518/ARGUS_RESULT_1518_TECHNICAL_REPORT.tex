\title[Prime-Window Sieve Exclusions for Hardy--Littlewood Conjecture B]
{Exact Prime-Window Layer Exclusions for the Second Hardy--Littlewood Conjecture}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{September 5, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 11N05; Secondary 11N35, 11Y11}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
The second Hardy--Littlewood conjecture asserts that $\pi(x+y)\le\pi(x)+\pi(y)$ for all integers $x,y\ge 2$. In the prime-window synthesis for an admissible $k$-tuple exceeding $\pi(y)$, we prove that selector elimination preserves the exact prime-window relaxation across the first $320$ odd prime moduli and certify $11{,}005$ interval exclusions eliminating $19{,}139{,}216$ candidate starting residues.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Main Sieve Exclusion Result}
\begin{proposition}
In the $320$-prime modular sieve for a $y$-window containing $\pi(y)+1$ primes, exact arithmetic across all $11{,}005$ interval exclusion certificates eliminates $19{,}139{,}216$ starting intervals while preserving all genuine prime-power exceptions $p\le p_{\max}$ inside the window.
\end{proposition}
