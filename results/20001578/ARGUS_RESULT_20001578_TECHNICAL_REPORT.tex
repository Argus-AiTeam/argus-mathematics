\begin{abstract}
In the investigation of finite F\o{}lner/amenability equality candidates in Thompson's group $F$ on the ring-shifted $p=22$ template, we prove that all $18$-element equality candidates are impossible: the ordered-grid balance requires adjacent rows to share at least $15$ right-quotient products, whereas exhaustive exact evaluation of the quotient table proves that the maximum negative-quotient multiplicity is~$8$.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Main Exclusion Theorem}
\begin{theorem}
In the $p=22$ two-column derived template inside Thompson's group $F$, any $18$-element configuration satisfying the boundary-cancellation equality between generators $x_0$ and $x_1$ requires adjacent row fibres to share at least $15$ common right quotients, whereas the complete exact quotient table has maximum off-diagonal quotient multiplicity $8 < 15$. Hence no $18$-element equality witness exists in this family.
\end{theorem}
