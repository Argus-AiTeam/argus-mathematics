# 20000332 Evidence

Disposition: published_refutation.

Accepted proposition branch: semistable limits for pluricanonical embeddings eventually stabilize when K_X is replaced by high enough powers. This row is scoped to Chow/log-Chow semistable limits, the formulation recorded in the ledger.

Primary source: Xiaowei Wang and Chenyang Xu, "Nonexistence of asymptotic GIT compactification", arXiv:1212.0173v2.

Read source locations:
- Abstract, page 1.
- Theorem 1, pages 2-3 in the PDF text extraction.

Evidence summary: the abstract says the authors give families, including smooth hypersurfaces in P^3 of large degree, such that Chow semistable limits under distinct pluricanonical embeddings do not stabilize. Theorem 1 gives projective flat families with different powers producing different Chow semistable behavior.
