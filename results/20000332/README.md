# Result 20000332

## Non-stabilization of Chow semistable pluricanonical limits

**Status:** independently reviewed primary-source refutation (Batch 001)<br>
**Field:** algebraic geometry and moduli stability

Xiaowei Wang and Chenyang Xu (*Ann. of Math.* 180, 2014; `arXiv:1205.5782`, Theorem 1.1) construct flat families (including smooth hypersurfaces in $\mathbb P^3$ of large degree) whose Chow semistable $r$-canonical limits do not stabilize as $r \to \infty$.
