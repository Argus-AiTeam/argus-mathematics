# Result 1200029

## Dead ends can have positive ball density in an infinite Cayley graph

**Status:** independently reviewed published counterexample  
**Originality:** published by Calegari and Fujiwara  
**Field:** geometric group theory and Cayley graph geometry  
**Live result:** <https://open.argusbot.cn/results/1200029>

Consider the hyperbolic triangle group

$$
\Gamma=\langle a,b\mid a^2,\ b^3,\ (ab)^7\rangle
$$

with symmetric generating set \(S=\{a,b,b^{-1}\}\). Calegari and Fujiwara
show that dead ends have positive asymptotic density in closed word balls,
with lower bound at least

$$
2^{-6}=\frac{1}{64}.
$$

The group is infinite because \(1/2+1/3+1/7<1\). Under the closed-ball
normalization used by the associated literature, this provides a
counterexample to the universal claim that dead ends have density zero in
every infinite Cayley graph.

## Boundary

The one-line problem does not define its density convention. This result does
not determine density in word spheres and does not claim that \(1/64\) is the
exact limiting value.
