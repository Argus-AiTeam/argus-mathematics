# Fujino v1: compact and divisor conventions

Read source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.11315v1.
The downloaded PDF metadata identifies arXiv:2201.11315v1, 27 January 2022,
86 pages. Saved as `fujino_2201.11315v1.pdf`, with a layout text extraction.
The following are faithful paraphrases, not extended verbatim quotations.

- Page 3, condition (P): normal source, Stein base, Stein compact W, and
  finitely many connected components of W intersect Z for every analytic
  subset Z defined on a neighborhood of W. A semianalytic Stein compact
  satisfies the last condition.
- Page 6, Section 1.11: inside a Stein space, an Oka--Weil domain and a
  semianalytic Stein compact can enclose a prescribed relatively compact
  neighborhood. Starting with a point compact permits enclosing the chosen
  curve core without first supposing that the core satisfies (P4).
- Page 11, Theorem 1.24: under (P), a projective morphism with a klt pair
  admits, after shrinking the base around W, a small projective
  bimeromorphic contraction from a normal variety Q-factorial over W.
  Crepancy is a separate consequence of smallness and the Q-Cartier
  canonical sheaf, not additional wording in the theorem.
- Page 22, Definition 2.32: analytic Weil divisors have locally finite
  support and globally defined closed irreducible prime components.
- Page 24, Definition 2.38: Q-factoriality at a compact tests prime
  divisors defined near the whole compact, at each point of that compact.
  Over W means at the full inverse image of W.
- Page 24, Remark 2.39: this property need not pass to a smaller compact,
  because a divisor near the smaller compact may not extend near the
  larger one.
- Page 25, Lemma 2.42: on a normal rational-singularity space, a finite
  prescribed list of integral Weil divisors Q-Cartier at a compact has
  one positive Cartier multiple on a neighborhood of the compact.
- Page 25, Remark 2.43: on a projective morphism to a Stein space,
  canonical and other rank-one reflexive sheaves have divisor
  representatives, using an ample twist and Cartan A.
- Pages 30-32, Remark 3.8 and Theorem 3.12: a relatively compact
  restriction of a klt pair is dlt in the stated convention; dlt spaces
  have rational singularities. Thus rationality is available locally.

The full-compact application in report Section 2 uses a finite list of
divisors defined before smaller-core restriction, as these statements
require. This source note is not independent Reviewer acceptance.
