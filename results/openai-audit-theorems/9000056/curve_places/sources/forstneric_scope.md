# Forstneric 2005: the ambient space may be singular

Read original journal scan:
https://www.numdam.org/item/AIF_2005__55_3_733_0.pdf.
Canonical identifier: `doi:10.5802/aif.2112`.
Saved as `forstneric_2005.pdf`, with a layout text extraction.

At pp. 735-736 (PDF pp. 4-5), complex spaces are assumed reduced and
paracompact. The hull paragraph transfers holomorphic convexity of a
compact between a closed complex subvariety and a Stein ambient space.

Theorem 2.1 assumes a closed Stein subvariety X0 of a complex space X,
and a compact K holomorphically convex in a Stein open neighborhood,
with K intersect X0 holomorphically convex in X0. It gives a basis of
Stein neighborhoods of K union X0. With K empty it gives a Stein
neighborhood of X0, as stated explicitly after the theorem.
The opening of the proof explicitly permits a singular X, even though
the author's immediate application only needs a nonsingular ambient.

These are paraphrases of the accessed passage. The input is closedness
and Steinness of the curve, not mere smoothness. The subsequent
Runge-core argument in the report is needed separately to obtain a
relatively compact Stein neighborhood inside a prescribed open.
This source reading is not independent mathematical review.
