# Statement fidelity: IntegralTwoSwitch

`Main.lean` formalizes the following algebraic parts of the integral
two-switch verification theorem. All declarations are in namespace
`IntegralTwoSwitch`. They use the displayed hypotheses and no axioms
beyond Lean and Mathlib's ordinary mathematical foundations.

- `derivative_telescoping` is the group-ring polynomial identity for
  every natural number n and every element s of any commutative ring.
  `cyclicDerivative` is the sum of i*s^i for 0 <= i < n and
  `cyclicNorm` is the sum of s^i over the same range.
  `cyclic_derivative_identity` specializes it under s^n=1. Applied to
  the commutative cyclic group algebra, n=ell+1 gives the manuscript's
  `rd:derivative-identity`. This is induction for arbitrary n, not a
  bounded test of cyclic orders.
- `exact_frobenius` proves the manuscript's `rd:exact-frobenius` in any
  commutative ring from f^2-a*f+ell=0. For a Frobenius endomorphism it
  is applied inside the commutative subalgebra generated by that
  endomorphism and the scalars. It is not a theorem about several
  arbitrary noncommuting endomorphisms.
- `cleared_lifts_agree`, `clearedDescent_restrict`,
  `clearedDescent_eq`, `clearedDescent_add`, and
  `clearedDescent_natural` prove simultaneous bounded-ambiguity
  descent for arbitrary additive abelian groups. Given a homomorphism
  r:A->B, a natural number h killing ker(r), and an h-multiple lift
  of every b, `clearedDescent` multiplies any such lift by h. The
  result is independent of the lift, is additive, restricts to
  h^2*b, and is natural in commuting squares with the stated
  hypotheses. No bound on a group order or number of primes occurs.
  These theorems do not assert that restriction in a particular
  Galois-cohomology problem has those hypotheses: that application
  is proved separately in the report using inflation-restriction
  and the bounded torsion coefficient module.
- `optimal_natural_clearing` proves an integer divisibility statement:
  if h is nonzero, h^3 divides h*x-q, and h^3 divides h^2*x, then
  h^2 divides q. In the report these are exactly the restriction
  and automorphism-invariance conditions on a putative natural
  q-cleared descent for multiplication by h on Z/(h^3). The
  interpretation as a sharpness example is supplied in prose;
  the divisibility implication itself is kernel checked.
- `coherent_diamond` checks all nine displayed matrix identities
  for every p in every commutative ring. The `strictD`, `relaxedD`,
  `finiteD`, and `singularD` definitions are the concrete two-term
  complexes of the report. The relative maps and their inverses
  and homotopy give an actual integral deformation retraction onto
  the shifted dual strict complex. The finite and singular pairing
  matrices satisfy their signed chain-map identities, and the
  local duality defect is exactly `localPair`, with zero pure
  planes. These are identities of complete matrices, not generic
  cohomology identifications. The normal-form arguments establishing
  the plane pairings' quasi-isomorphisms and the determinant
  coordinates are checked separately by `verify.py` and proved
  in the report. This matrix family is not asserted to arise
  from a particular elliptic curve.
- `transferred_boundary` is the exact additive comparison identity
  for a specified cycle-contraction witness and a commuting square.
  It proves the retained boundary formula, not existence of a
  contraction of arbitrary Galois cochains.
- `two_by_two_volume` proves the determinant formula for the action
  of an arbitrary 2-by-2 matrix on two vectors over any commutative
  ring. `nonprimitive_volume` proves that v=p*(a,b) has volume one
  with x=(-s/p,r/p), for every nonzero p in any field, provided
  a*r+b*s=1. Over a DVR this applies to arbitrary localization
  divisibility p, with no bound on its valuation.
- `unit_product_no_loss` and `arbitrary_switch_count` prove
  conservation for an arbitrary finite list of zero-valuation
  factors, for any multiplicatively additive valuation map to an
  additive commutative monoid. The name `units` denotes that list;
  invertibility is not an implicit Lean hypothesis. In the
  application its entries are actual integral units, whose
  valuations are zero.

The compiler does not verify the report's derived determinant-functor
argument, the diamond's normal-form determinant calculation,
the Tate-image scalar theorem, the existence of the Heegner points and
their trace/reduction relations, the local unramified descent theorem,
or the arithmetic realization and compatibility of every global
Selmer diagram. Those are explicitly separated in the report. In
particular, this formalization is not a formal proof of the companion's
pointwise converse, of the rational manuscript's undecidability theorem,
or of arithmetic applicability without the listed hypotheses.
