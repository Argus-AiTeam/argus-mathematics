# Result 20000496

## Smooth centrally symmetric convex domains in high dimensions with interior hot spots

**Status:** independently reviewed primary-source refutation (Batch 001)<br>
**Field:** spectral geometry and partial differential equations

Jaume de Dios Pont (`arXiv:2402.13974`, Theorem 1.1) constructs smooth centrally symmetric convex domains in $\mathbb R^d$ (for sufficiently large $d$) whose second Neumann eigenfunction attains its strict maximum in the interior rather than on the boundary.
