\title[Exact Exclusion of Cross-Block Voltage Families for the Erd\H{o}s--Gy\'arf\'as Conjecture]
{Exact Exclusion of $43{,}046{,}721$ Cross-Block Voltage Graphs for the Erd\H{o}s--Gy\'arf\'as $2^k$-Cycle Conjecture}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{September 5, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 05C38; Secondary 05C25}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
The Erd\H{o}s--Gy\'arf\'as conjecture asserts that every graph of minimum degree at least~$3$ contains a simple cycle of length a power of~$2$. We prove that in the cross-block spoke voltage family over $\mathbb{Z}_{32}$, every one of the $3^{16}=43{,}046{,}721$ voltage assignments contains a simple cycle of length $8$, $16$, or $32$, covered by nine explicit cycle witnesses without symmetry reduction.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Main Family Exclusion}
\begin{theorem}
Across the $3^{16}=43{,}046{,}721$ cubic voltage lifts in the cross-block spoke family over $\mathbb{Z}_{32}$, nine explicit closed voltage walk templates lift to simple cycles of length $8$, $16$, or $32$ that partition the entire assignment space, proving that no graph in this family is a counterexample to the Erd\H{o}s--Gy\'arf\'as conjecture.
\end{theorem}
