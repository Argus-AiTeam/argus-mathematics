\title[A Characteristic-Two Three-Dimensional plt Pair With Non-Normal Centre]
{A Characteristic-Two $\mathbb{Q}$-Factorial Three-Dimensional plt Pair With a Non-Normal Divisorial Centre}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{August 27, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 14E30; Secondary 14G17}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We document the counterexample of Cascini and Tanaka showing that in dimension three and characteristic $p=2$, a $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial purely log terminal (plt) pair $(Z,E)$ with $E$ a prime divisor can have $E$ non-normal.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Main Refutation}
\begin{theorem}[Cascini--Tanaka]
Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p=2$, there exists a projective $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial three-dimensional variety $Z$ and a prime divisor $E\subset Z$ such that the pair $(Z,E)$ is purely log terminal (plt), yet $E$ is not normal.
\end{theorem}
