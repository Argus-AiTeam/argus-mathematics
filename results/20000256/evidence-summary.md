# 20000256 Evidence

Disposition: published_refutation.

Accepted proposition: in a purely log terminal pair (X,D=S+B), the coefficient-one divisor S is necessarily normal.

Primary source: Paolo Cascini and Hiromu Tanaka, "Purely log terminal threefolds with non-normal centres in characteristic two", arXiv:1607.08590v2.

Read source locations:
- Abstract, page 1.
- Introduction and Theorem 1.1, page 2.

Evidence summary: the abstract announces a three-dimensional plt pair whose codimension-one part is not normal. Theorem 1.1 gives an algebraically closed field of characteristic two and a Q-factorial three-dimensional plt pair (Z,E) over that field such that E is a prime divisor and is not normal.

Verification: source-theorem check only; no explicit finite witness or Jacobian operation applies.
