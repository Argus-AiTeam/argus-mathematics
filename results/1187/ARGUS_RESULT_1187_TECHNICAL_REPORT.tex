{Collapse of the Nielsen-Torsion Quotient on $G_{4,3}$ and Strict $\mathrm{AC}_3$ Certificates in $F/K^{(3)}$}

\begin{abstract}
In the investigation of potential counterexamples to the Andrews--Curtis conjecture, we establish two exact group-theoretic non-separation theorems: (1)~on the nilpotent quotient $G_{4,3}=F_4/[F_4^3,F_4]$, the realizable $\mathrm{AC}_3$ determinant locus coincides with the full unit locus $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{AC3}}=\mathcal{R}_U$, collapsing the component-conjugation torsion invariant for all generating tuples; and (2)~in the untruncated third-derived quotient $F/K^{(3)}$, explicit finite strict Andrews--Curtis programs reduce both candidate presentations $P_0$ and $P_2$ to the trivial kernel representative.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Main Theorems}
\begin{theorem}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
  \item Let $G_{4,3}=F_4/[F_4^3,F_4]$. Under the component-conjugation determinant map, every unit class in $\mathcal{R}_U$ is realized by an $\mathrm{AC}_3$-equivalent generating tuple: $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{AC3}}=\mathcal{R}_U$.
  \item For the two benchmark balanced presentations $P_0$ and $P_2$, explicit finite sequences of strict Andrews--Curtis moves map both tuples to the trivial representative in $F/K^{(3)}$, where $K^{(3)}$ is the third term of the derived series of the normal closure $K$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
