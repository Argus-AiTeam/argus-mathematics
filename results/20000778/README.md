# Result 20000778

## A universal phantom subcategory on the blow-up of $\mathbb P^2_{\mathbb C}$ at 10 general points

**Status:** independently reviewed primary-source refutation (Batch 001)<br>
**Field:** algebraic geometry and derived categories

Johannes Krah (*Invent. Math.* 235, 2024; `arXiv:2301.11602`, Theorem 1.1) constructs an exceptional collection of line bundles on the blow-up of $\mathbb P^2_{\mathbb C}$ at 10 general points whose orthogonal complement in $D^b(X)$ is a universal phantom category.
