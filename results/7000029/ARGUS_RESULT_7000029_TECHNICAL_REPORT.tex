\title[Smooth Surfaces With Isolated Umbilic Index Exceeding One]
{Smooth Counterexamples to the $C^\infty$ Loewner and Carath\'eodory Umbilic-Index Conjectures}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{August 27, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 53A05; Secondary 37E35, 58K45}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
Problem~8.2 in Ghomi's problem list on geometry of curves and surfaces asks to show that the index of any isolated singularity of a principal line field on a surface is at most~$1$ (Loewner's conjecture). While Hamburger proved the real-analytic case, we document and verify the $C^\infty$ counterexample family $g_k$ of Alp\"oge (2026) and its Lean~4 formalization, which constructs a $C^\infty$ surface germ at the origin with an isolated umbilic of index $1+k/2 = 3/2 > 1$ at $k=1$ (and a smooth convex body with a single umbilic of index~$2$ at $k=2$).
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Summary of the Verified Formal Theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Alp\"oge, 2026; Lean 4 verified]
In the $C^\infty$ category:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
  \item For every integer $k\ge 1$, there exists a $C^\infty$ function $g_k:\mathbb{R}^2\to\mathbb{R}$ whose trace-free Hessian $\bigl(\partial_{xx}g_k - \partial_{yy}g_k,\ 2\partial_{xy}g_k\bigr)$ has an isolated zero at the origin with winding number $2+k$, corresponding to a principal-line umbilic index of $1 + k/2 > 1$.
  \item Taking $k=1$ gives an isolated $C^\infty$ umbilic of index $3/2 > 1$, refuting the $C^\infty$ Loewner conjecture.
  \item Taking $k=2$ and extending to $S^2$ yields the support function of a $C^\infty$ strictly convex body in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with a unique umbilic point (of index~$2$), refuting the $C^\infty$ Carath\'eodory conjecture.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
