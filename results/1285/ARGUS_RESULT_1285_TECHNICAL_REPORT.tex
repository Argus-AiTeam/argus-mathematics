\title[Lower Bounds for Restricted CRT-Local and Dual-Chain Factoring Models]
{Asymptotic Success Upper Bounds for Restricted CRT-Local Factoring Controllers and ECM Dual-Chain Counterfamilies}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{September 5, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 11Y05; Secondary 68Q17}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We prove two unconditional negative theorems against restricted deterministic integer-factoring controller models: (1)~for one-gcd CRT-local controllers equipped with equality and Jacobi-symbol feedback, an infinite balanced-semiprime family limits asymptotic success to at most $5/8+o(1)$; and (2)~for the elliptic-curve dual-chain exponent model with resource parameters $(c,e)$, a shell-uniform Rankin and prime-selection construction produces an infinite balanced-semiprime counterfamily with exponent parameter $\eta=1/(256\max(3c+2,e+1))$.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Main Theorems}
\begin{theorem}
Let $N=pq$ range over balanced semiprimes.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
  \item Every deterministic controller in the frozen one-gcd CRT-local grammar with equality and Jacobi-symbol branching succeeds on a fraction at most $5/8 + o(1)$ of balanced semiprimes as $N\to\infty$.
  \item For every fixed positive rational resource tuple $(c,e)$ in the input-specific ECM dual-chain model, there exists an infinite family of balanced semiprimes $N=pq$ on which the dual-chain point multiples return to the identity $\mathcal{O}$ simultaneously across both prime factors without separating $p$ and $q$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
