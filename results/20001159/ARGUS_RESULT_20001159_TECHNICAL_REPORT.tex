\title[The Balanced Circulant $K_{3,3}$ and the Corrected $G_B(k,m)$ Classification]
{A Connected Counterexample $K_{3,3}$ to the AIM Balanced-Circulant Conjecture and the Corrected Classification}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{August 27, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 05C17; Secondary 05C25, 05C75}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
Section~K.1 of the AIM perfect-graph problem list conjectures that every nonempty balanced circulant graph is isomorphic to $G_B(k,m)=C_{4m}[\overline{K_k}]$ on $4km$ vertices. We verify that the connected circulant $K_{3,3}=\mathrm{Circ}(6;\{1,3\})$ on $6$ vertices is balanced (all $9$ induced cycles have length~$4$) yet cannot have $4km$ vertices, and confirm by exhaustive enumeration of all $196{,}573$ nonempty circulants of order $n\le 32$ that the $106$ balanced circulants agree exactly with the Morris--Spiga--Webb classification ($G_B(k,m)$ with $m\ge 2$ or $K_{t,t}$ with $t\ge 1$ on each connected component).
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Main Proposition}
\begin{theorem}
The circulant graph $G = \mathrm{Circ}(6;\{1,3\})$ on $\mathbb{Z}_6$ satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
  \item $G$ is connected, $3$-regular, vertex-transitive, and bipartite with parts $\{0,2,4\}$ and $\{1,3,5\}$, hence isomorphic to $K_{3,3}$.
  \item Every induced cycle of $G$ has length $4\equiv 0\pmod 4$ (with zero induced $C_6$), so $G$ is balanced.
  \item Since $|V(G)|=6$ is not divisible by $4$, $G$ is not isomorphic to $G_B(k,m)$ for any positive integers $k,m$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
