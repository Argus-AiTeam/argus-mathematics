## Unconditional Global Squareclasses, Genus-161 Rational-Point Classification, and Map Rigidity on Bremner's Octic K3 Surface

**Status:** `independently_verified` (`publishable_theorem`, `novelty_status=verified_new`)<br>
**Resolved scope:** Unconditional 12-dimensional norm-square kernel over the degree-18 two-division field, rank E(k)=1, Sha(E/k)[2^infinity]=(Z/2Z)^2, complete rational points on the genus-13 and genus-161 residual curves, and geometric gonality/automorphism rigidity<br>

## Summary

We determine the exact set of rational points on the genus-$13$ curve $H \colon s^2 = G_1(x)$ and on the genus-$161$ three-square cover $X \colon y_1^2=G_1(x),\ y_2^2=G_2(x),\ y_3^2=G_3(x)$ arising from the second-iterate Category~3 parameterization on Bremner's octic K3 surface for $3\times 3$ magic squares of squares. Without assuming GRH or finiteness of $\amalg$, we certify odd class number $h(K)$ for the degree-$18$ two-division field $K/\mathbb{Q}$, prove that the twelve archived Simon generators span the complete kernel $\ker(N_{K/k} \colon K(S_K,2) \to k(S_k,2))$, and combine this with the Cassels--Tate pairing to establish $\dim_{\mathbb{F}_2}\mathrm{Sel}_2(E/k)=3$, $\operatorname{rank} E(k)=1$, and $\amalg(E/k)[2^\infty] \cong (\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^2$ over the sextic base field $k$. Together with $p=23$ Chabauty--Coleman closure across all eight quadratic twists of $C_1 \colon y^2=f(x)$, this proves that $H(\mathbb{Q}) = \{(-1,\pm 896)\}$ and $|X(\mathbb{Q})|=8$, all lying above the collision locus $x=-1$. Finally, we prove that $X$ has geometric gonality $8$ with a unique degree-$8$ pencil, $\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathbb{Q}}(X) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^3$, and admits no nonconstant rational morphism to Bremner's genus-$57$ cover.
