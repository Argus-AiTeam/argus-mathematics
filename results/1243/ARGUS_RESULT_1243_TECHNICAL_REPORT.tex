\title[An Explicit Cassels--Tate Pairing for the Magic Square of Squares]
{An Explicit Nondegenerate Cassels--Tate Pairing on a Genus-Two Cover for the Magic Square of Squares}
\author{Argus Open Research Project}
\date{September 5, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 11G30; Secondary 11G05, 11D41}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For the genus-two cover associated with the $3\times 3$ magic square of squares branch, we compute and independently verify an explicit order-12 global norm preimage, smooth Cassels conic point, and the local Hilbert symbols across all six bad finite places, establishing the nontrivial Cassels--Tate pairing value $\langle 936, 1304\rangle_{\mathrm{CT}} = 1/2\pmod{1}$.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
\section{Main Theorem}
\begin{theorem}
In the $2$-Selmer descent on the branch-483 elliptic/genus-two cover over the degree-18 number field $K$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
  \item There exist explicit polynomial-modulus elements $C,D\in K$ satisfying the exact norm identity $C^2 - y D^2 = b$ unconditionally (verified by direct polynomial division without assuming unconditional class-group completeness).
  \item The transported point on the Cassels conic has $T\neq 0$, normalized to $T=1$, with nonvanishing gradient and verified tangent linear form.
  \item Across the six ramified/bad finite places of the cover, the product of the local Hilbert symbols for the residual Selmer classes $(2759, 936)$ equals $-1$, where $2759 = 1304 + 4063$ and $4063$ is the Kummer image of the known Mordell--Weil generator $Q$. Since $Q$ lies in the radical of the Cassels--Tate pairing, bilinearity yields
  \[
    \langle 936,\ 1304\rangle_{\mathrm{CT}} \;=\; \frac{1}{2} \pmod{1}.
  \]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{remark}
Conditional on the displayed three-dimensional local intersection exhausting the global $2$-Selmer group (which holds under GRH for the degree-36 field extension), Theorem~1.1 forces $\mathrm{rank}\,E(K)=1$ and closes the corresponding rational-point branch via the $p=23$ Coleman/formal-logarithm disk certificate.
\end{remark}
