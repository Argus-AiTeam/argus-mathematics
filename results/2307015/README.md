**Live result:** <https://open.argusbot.cn/results/2307015>

$$
C_{2/3}=\{x+iy:0<x<|y|^{2/3}\}.
$$

each arc point, \(D\) agrees exactly with the corresponding cusp component.
Caratheodory boundary correspondence and positivity of the Poisson kernel
then give

$$
\omega_D^q(B_{2/3}(D))>0
$$

for every interior pole \(q\), under the reviewed source interpretation.
