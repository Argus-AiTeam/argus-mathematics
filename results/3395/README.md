# Result 3395

## Centered Nuclear Duality, Addition-Aware Pinning, and Relative-Mass Obstructions in Monotone Boolean Circuit Lower Bounds

**Status:** `independently_verified` (`scoped_obstruction_theorems`, `novelty_status=verified_new`)<br>
**Resolved scope:** Two-sided equivalence between fixed-factor Boolean cube approximation and centered nuclear-error charge, addition-aware pinning decomposition, signed-test transfer reduction, and explicit O(M_n+N^2)-gate refutations of uncentered relative-mass and matrix-only gluing shortcuts<br>
**Field:** computational complexity and circuit lower bounds

## Summary

In the study of monotone and positive-weight Boolean circuit lower bounds for fixed-factor approximation of $R_n = 2F_G - T_G$ over the full Boolean cube, we establish a two-sided equivalence between constant-factor circuit approximation bounds and a centered nuclear-error dual charge, prove an addition-aware pinning decomposition and fixed-tolerance signed-test transfer reduction, and construct explicit $O(M_n+N^2)$-gate nonnegative rational circuits and affine-line incidence matrices over $\mathbb{F}_{4^\ell}$ refuting uncentered relative-mass, naive rarity-budget, and sampler-free matrix-gluing shortcuts.
