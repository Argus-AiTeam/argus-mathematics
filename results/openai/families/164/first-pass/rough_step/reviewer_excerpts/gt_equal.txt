Source: Green and Tao, arXiv:1311.6170v3, Introduction, corrected
[GT, Theorem 8.6], and Section 2's definition of the smoothness norm.
Primary page independently read: https://arxiv.org/html/1311.6170v3

Quoted theorem, with mathematical typography normalized to ASCII:
"Let 0 < delta < 1/2, and let m,t >= 1, N_1,...,N_t >= 1 and d >= 1
be integers. Write vec N = (N_1,...,N_t) and
[vec N] = [N_1] x ... x [N_t]. Suppose that G/Gamma is an
m-dimensional nilmanifold equipped with a 1/delta-rational Mal'cev
basis X adapted to some filtration G_bullet of degree d, and that
g in poly(Z^t,G_bullet). Then either (g(vec n)Gamma)_{vec n in [vec N]}
is delta-equidistributed, or N_i << delta^(-O_{d,m,t}(1)) for some
i=1,...,t, or else there is some horizontal character eta with
0 < ||eta|| << delta^(-O_{d,m,t}(1)) such that
||eta o g||_{C^infinity[vec N]} << delta^(-O_{d,m,t}(1)).

In the 'equal sides' case N_1 = ... = N_t, the alternative
N_i << delta^(-O_{d,m,t}(1)) may be deleted."

The displayed smoothness norm is
sup_{vec i != 0} vec N^(vec i) ||alpha_{vec i}||_{R/Z},
where f(vec n) = sum_{vec i} alpha_{vec i} binom(vec n,vec i).
