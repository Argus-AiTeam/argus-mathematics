Source: Green and Tao, arXiv:0709.3562v4, Lemma A.2(i), its proof,
and Lemma A.3. Read in the retained primary-source HTML.

Quoted Lemma A.2(i), with mathematical typography normalized:
"Let X be a basis for g with the nesting property that
[g,X_i] subset Span(X_{i+1},...,X_m)
for i=1,...,m-1. Then the compositions psi_exp o psi^{-1} and
psi o psi_exp^{-1} are both polynomial maps on R^m with degree O(1).
If X is Q-rational then all the coefficients of these polynomials
are rational of height at most Q^{O(1)}."

The proof displays
log(exp(X)exp(Y)) =
X + Y + [X,Y]/2 + [X,[X,Y]]/12 - [Y,[X,Y]]/12 + ...
and continues:
"this expression being a sum of O_s(1) terms, each of which is a
rational number of height O_s(1) times a commutator of order at most s
involving Xs and Ys."

Lemma A.3, under the same nesting property, gives triangular
polynomial formulas for multiplication and inversion in second-kind
coordinates. For Q >= 2 and a Q-rational basis, all their coefficients
are rational of height Q^{O(1)}.
