## Exact target and reference version

Let X be a smooth simply connected complex projective contact Fano manifold
of dimension 2n+1, n>=1, with its given sequence
`0 -> F -> T_X --theta--> L -> 0`, `Pic(X)=Z[L]`, and a family of free
L-degree-one maps covering a dense open. Put `Z=X x X`, `M=L boxtimes L`.
For **every proper integral algebraic space Y over C**, every dominant
`p:Y -> Z`, and everywhere regular

    W12 in H0(Y,p*(T_X boxtimes L)),
    W21 in H0(Y,p*(L boxtimes T_X))

with equal contact value
`xi=(theta boxtimes 1)W12=(1 boxtimes theta)W21`, the assertion is existence
of exactly one `B in H0(Z,M)` with `p*B=xi`.

No normality, dimension, projectivity or fiber condition on Y, and no
finiteness or flatness of p, is added. Both lifts are essential to the proof's
two factor types. Neither a moving-pair construction nor classification is
an assumption of this proposition.

## Decisive argument assessment

### 1. Finite image without normalizing Y

The contact value defines a proper map `Y -> Tot(M)`: the graph is closed
and the source is proper over C. Its reduced image S0 is an integral
**scheme**, since it is closed in the line-bundle total space. Reducedness
of Y gives factorization through S0. The proof correctly justifies
properness of S0 even for algebraic-space Y by universal closedness descending
along the universally surjective proper map `Y -> S0`.

Thus `q0:S0 -> Z` is affine, proper and finite type, hence finite. It is
dominant and surjective. Finite-type-over-C is the Nagata hypothesis making
the normalization `S -> S0` finite. Characteristic zero supplies a common
dense open U of Z over which `S=S0` and the finite map q is etale.
The finite images of both excluded closed loci have smaller dimension,
so removing them really leaves a dense open.

The important possible loophole is absent: the proof **never requires a
map Y -> S**, which need not exist for nonnormal Y. Standard scheme results
about normalization are applied to S0 and S, not indiscriminately to Y.
This supports C_DES_FINITE.

### 2. Every generic sheet, not a selected rational point

For a test line `c=(f,y)` meeting U, take every dominating reduced
irreducible component D of `P1 x_Z Y`. D is a proper integral algebraic
space over C, so `H0(D,O_D)=C` by the explicit proper-function argument in
section 3. Projecting W12 to the trivial quotient of f*T_X makes that
projection a constant vector. Since theta kills the positive subbundle,
the contact value on D is the pullback of a section a_D on P1.
For `c=(y,f)`, W21 gives the same conclusion.

Write K=C(P1). The generic finite image algebra A is reduced and etale
because c meets U. Crucially, A is a quotient of **K[zeta]**: the finite
image lives in a line bundle, so its algebra is generated by the contact
coordinate. On each D that coordinate is a scalar a_D in K. Surjectivity
onto the finite image therefore makes **every** residue field of A equal
to K. The Chinese remainder theorem yields `A=K^r`.

This does not assume D has a K-point: the scalar coordinate determines
its image, not all its functions. Nor does it require connected or reduced
fibers. Every dominating reduced curve component of `P1 x_Z S` consequently
has function field K and finite degree-one normalization over P1.
Vertical or embedded components elsewhere are not silently excluded.
This is the decisive complete all-sheet argument for C_DES_SHEETS.

### 3. A line detects any possible branch divisor

For a prime divisor Delta in X x X, first suppose its second projection is
dominant. Properness makes it surjective; a general fiber Delta_y is a
nonzero effective Cartier divisor in X. Since `Pic(X)=Z[L]` and L is ample,
`O(Delta_y)=L^a` with a>0, by positive intersection with `L^(dim X-1)`.
Every contact line not contained in that divisor has positive intersection
degree a. Lines `(f,y)` thus meet it.

Otherwise dimension and properness force
`Delta=X x D` for a prime divisor D in X. The opposite factor type
`(y,f)` detects it by the same positive-degree argument.

For either type the universal evaluation `E:P x P1 -> Z` is smooth
dominant with irreducible P. A codimension-at-least-two closed exceptional
set has inverse-image dimension at most `dim P-1`; projection to P is
closed because P1 is proper. General parameters therefore avoid it.
This same estimate allows any additional prescribed closed codimension-two
exceptional set, as stated in the graph.

The inverse image of a prescribed dense smooth open Delta0 is smooth of
dimension dim P. Each dominating component is generically etale over P in
characteristic zero. Removing the closures of non-dominating images and
non-etale images leaves a dense open where all intersections are transverse.
Meeting U and not lying in Delta are nonempty open conditions. Their
intersection with these other dense opens is nonempty; positive intersection
ensures that an intersection with Delta actually remains.

Thus smooth **dominant**, rather than everywhere-surjective, evaluation
is enough. No general-position assertion is made for just one preselected
line or only a general divisor. This supports C_DES_DETECTION.

### 4. A transverse pullback detects every ramification index

For a putative ramified prime divisor R of normal S, shrink a dense smooth
open Delta0 in its image Delta. Finite maps preserve dimension of closed
subsets; normality gives regularity in codimension one; and characteristic
zero gives generic separability. These facts allow removal of singular
points, intersections of the finitely many divisors over Delta, and their
non-etale tangential loci. Set-theoretically every point over Delta lies on
one of those height-one divisors, since a nonzero principal ideal in the
integral source has height-one minimal primes.

At each remaining point above Delta0 both source and divisor are smooth.
A local defining equation pulls back as a unit times `z1^e`.
An analytic e-th root absorbs the unit, and etaleness along the divisor
makes the other pulled-back target coordinates into source coordinates.
The local map is

    (z1,z2,...,zN) -> (z1^e,z2,...,zN).

Ramification at R means e>1; index one and the separable tangential map are
unramified here. The test line from the preceding step meets Delta0
transversely and U generically. At a selected point above the intersection,
the actual pullback germ is

    C{z1,t}/(z1^e-a(t)),  a(0)=0, a'(0) != 0.

The derivative in t is nonzero, so this is a smooth irreducible curve germ
with t of order e in z1. Comparison with the completed algebraic local
ring shows the unique reduced algebraic curve component is regular there;
normalization is an isomorphism locally. Its projection is nonconstant,
so the component dominates P1.

But the all-sheet result makes its normalization finite of degree one
over normal P1, hence an isomorphism. A base uniformizer then has order
one, contradicting e>1. This supports C_DES_UNRAMIFIED for **all** indices,
not merely e=3.

The optional restatement `e*ord(z1)=1` is exactly the scope of the Lean
integer-order theorem. The geometric proof already obtains the contradiction
from the degree-one normalization; it does not need the cubic computation
or a mechanically constructed valuation. The source of order one is the
degree-one sheet and transversality, not an assumption smuggled into Lean.

### 5. Purity and global descent, including nonclosed points

For finite integral `A subset B`, with A regular and therefore normal,
going down and incomparability give equal heights at every source point
and its image, not just closed points. Nongeneric points have positive
local dimension. The source local ring is normal; the target local ring is
regular; q is quasi-finite; and all height-one generizations are unramified
by the preceding argument. This includes the point itself when its local
dimension is one. These are precisely the cited purity lemma's hypotheses.
The generic point is etale by separability. Thus q is finite etale
everywhere; flatness is a conclusion, not an earlier hypothesis.

S is projective, since it is finite over projective Z. Degree-zero GAGA
turns any hypothetical analytic clopen decomposition into an algebraic
idempotent, impossible for integral S. Its analytification is therefore
connected. Properness and etaleness give an analytic finite covering of
the simply connected manifold `Z^an=X^an x X^an`. A connected covering
has one sheet, so q has degree one.

Normalization is birational, so q0 is finite birational to normal Z.
It is an isomorphism without needing S0 normal. The tautological section
of the line bundle restricts to the required global B. Dominance of p
injects rational sections after local trivialization, proving uniqueness.
This supports C_DES_PURITY and the exact C_DES_DESCENT conclusion.
