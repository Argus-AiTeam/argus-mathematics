The surrounding setup takes Y,Y' smooth of constant dimension, v with
finite unramified diagonal, and X,X' separated Deligne-Mumford stacks.
Proposition 7.2 assumes the displayed square is Cartesian, a perfect
relative obstruction theory E -> L_(X/Y), and global resolutions. It gives
v^![X,E]=[X',u^*E] in the stated cases: v flat or a regular local immersion.
These qualifications are part of the cited result, not dispensable.

Proposition 7.3:
"Let E^bullet be a perfect relative obstruction theory for X over Y such
that h^0(E^bullet) is locally free. Assume that E^bullet has global
resolutions and X is a separated Deligne-Mumford stack, so that the virtual
fundamental class [X,E^bullet] exists."

Part (1):
"If h^(-1)(E^bullet)=0, then X is smooth over Y and [X,E^bullet]=[X]."
