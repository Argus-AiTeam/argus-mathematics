Source: Anca M. Mustata and Andrei Mustata,
The Chow ring of Mbar_(0,m)(P^n,d).
Version: arxiv:math/0507464v5.
URL opened: https://arxiv.org/html/math/0507464v5
and, by the separate source reader, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0507464v5 .
Location: section 1 setup, Definition 1.1 and Proposition 1.7.

The setup fixes g,m>=0, a>0, 0<=a_j<=1 and sum a_j+d*a>2-2g,
over a field of characteristic zero or characteristic greater than d.
The maps satisfy the specified weighted stability, smooth basepoint
support and weighted collision/basepoint-length conditions.

Proposition 1.7:
"The moduli problem of genus g, (A,a)-stable degree d nodal maps with m
marked points into P^n is finely represented by a proper Deligne-Mumford
stack Mbar_(g,A)(P^n,d,a). When g=0, the stack is smooth."

Notation and line wrapping are normalized to ASCII. The target is P^n;
this is not smoothness, effectivity or a virtual pushforward theorem for
arbitrary X. The old proof uses a direct construction on the ambient
projective-space stack and pulls its resulting data back to X-valued maps.
