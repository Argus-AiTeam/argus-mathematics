The theorem concerns Artin stacks of finite type over a base field.
Part (ii) identifies naive and Kresch Chow groups after tensoring with Q
for Deligne-Mumford stacks. Part (v) says A_j X=0 for j>dim X.

Part (iv):
"For any stack X with closed substack Z and complement U, the excision
sequence A_j Z -> A_j X -> A_j U -> 0 is exact."
Proposition 2.4.1 states this same closed/open exact sequence.

The theorem's covariance is for projective morphisms, not for every
nonrepresentable proper morphism. Section 4.3 explicitly says:
"the map X -> Spec k is proper ... but non-representable, so we do not
get a pushforward in the A_* theory."

That paragraph then passes to rational naive Chow groups to integrate a
complete DM stack, with possible additional stabilizer denominators.
The old proof correctly requires rational DM proper pushforward from
the separately cited Vistoli/coarse-moduli formalism.
