## Decisive source comparison

The archived independent excerpt and the primary HTML retrieved in this
review both give the admissibility term

    integral_gamma rho ds
      + sum_{p in C(G), |gamma| intersect p nonempty} rho(p) >= 1,

and state that `|gamma|` is the path's image. The index is the collection
of complementary components, not parameter times, so repeated visits to
one component do not repeat its term.

Proposition 4.2 assumes a countably connected domain in the sphere.
The boundary of its topological rectangle is contained in the domain,
with edges `J1,...,J4` in cyclic order; path families connect opposite
projected edges in the projected rectangle. It concludes the product
of the two transboundary moduli is 1. The immediately following paragraph
states that duality does not hold without countability and discusses
positive-area Cantor complements.

In contrast, the manuscript path cost is a sum over all dust
**visit times**; allowedness retains tags, earlier laws, residual
incidences and tuple exclusions, and the barrier must preserve
compatibility (P). The classical proposition neither constructs that
barrier nor establishes those conditions. This does not rule out
every conceivable comparison theorem; it rules out treating the
displayed classical result as an already supplied proof of this
stronger/different conclusion.

Consequently old route `R_CLASSICAL_IMPORT` was retired for a concrete
reason: different charge indexing and a countability restriction not
present in the proposed tagged theorem. Its failure is not a
refutation of Rajala, the OpenAI manuscript, or the direct barrier
route. The survey's description of an open conjecture in its own
2024 version is also not a verdict on a September 2026 manuscript.
The familiar annular formula `2*pi/log(R/r)` in Section 1 equation
(5) is a classical comparison, not proof of novelty of its application.
