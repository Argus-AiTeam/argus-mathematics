# Chevalley source passage

Source: The Stacks Project, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/054K,
Tag 054K, Theorem 29.23.3, retrieved 2026-11-05.

> Let \(f:X\to Y\) be a morphism of schemes. Assume \(f\) is
> quasi-compact and locally of finite presentation. Then the image
> of every locally constructible subset is locally constructible.

In the inner-removal proof, source and target are finite-type affine
varieties over an algebraically closed field, and the orbit map is
a finite-type morphism. The quasi-compact and finite-presentation
hypotheses are therefore present. On this Noetherian quasi-compact
target the image is constructible.
