# Dowd-Sin source passages

Source: Michael F. Dowd and Peter Sin, *On representations of algebraic
groups in characteristic two*, Communications in Algebra 24 (1996),
2597-2686, DOI 10.1080/00927879608542648.
The locators refer to the author version:
https://people.clas.ufl.edu/sin/files/representations-of-algebraic-groups-in-characteristic-2.pdf

The following is a mathematical transcription from I.3.1 and I.3.2.
The full obtained source and its OCR are `dowd_sin.pdf` and
`dowd_sin.txt`. Untilded objects have type \(B_\ell\), tilded objects
type \(C_\ell\); both groups are simply connected in characteristic two.

**I.3.1, equations (1)-(2).** The isogenies \(\sigma,\tau\) send a
long-root parameter \(z\) to \(z\) and a short-root parameter \(z\)
to \(z^2\), in the dual root system. The text states
\(\tau\circ\sigma=F_G\) and
\(\sigma\circ\tau=F_{\widetilde G}\).

**I.3.2, equations (1)-(2).** On fundamental weights,
\[
 \sigma:\widetilde\omega_i\mapsto\omega_i\ (i<\ell),
       \quad\widetilde\omega_\ell\mapsto2\omega_\ell,
 \qquad
 \tau:\omega_i\mapsto2\widetilde\omega_i\ (i<\ell),
       \quad\omega_\ell\mapsto\widetilde\omega_\ell .
\]
The composites of these injective weight maps are multiplication by two.
These are pullback weight maps, not maps in the same variance direction
as the group morphisms.
