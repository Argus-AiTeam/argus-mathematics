zero tails have trivial determinant. Let \(H\) denote all the appropriate

arbitrary nonalgebraic lines for the global comparison, reducible

## Decisive proof, including both equalities

The local-tail unit proves pointwise semipositivity and
\[
 \ker\Theta_{P_c}
       =\ker(\Theta_{L_0}+\Theta_{L_1})
\]
on the entire constant-rank variation locus. This yields the **second**
numerical equality once the boundary mixed-mass theorem is applied.
It is not restricted to orbit-filling points.

To compare with \(H\), choose a Hodge-generic point in the simultaneous
maximum-rank opens for the relevant forms and projective line map.
For a null direction \(v\), local two-tail reasoning gives
\(A^*G_1=0,\ A\ell=0\), zero projective flat-line derivative,
and zero compact-mark derivative.
Use the algebraic closure of the local line fiber. The orbit unit
gives a rational normal monodromy factor \(H_{\rm mon}\), constant
complementary whole Lie period, and suitable points at which its orbit
is filled. The notation \(H_{\rm mon}\) here is a group, not the marked
line \(H\).

Writing \(\ell_z=\mathbb Cs\), one has
\[
 t(v)\in\mathfrak h^{-1,1},\qquad
 \mathfrak h^{-1,1}s\subseteq G_1.
\]
For \(x=t(v)^*\), the local annihilation gives
\([x,y]s=0\) for **every** \(y\in\mathfrak h^{-1,1}\), not just \(x^*\).
The root-kernel theorem therefore gives \(t(v)E=0\). Together with the
compact-mark derivative this proves
\(\ker\Theta_{L_0+L_1}\subseteq\ker\Theta_H\) at those suitable points.
The same line fiber works for all null directions at that point.

Every nonempty subgerm of the fiber remains algebraically dense, so
these points can be found without leaving the maximum-rank opens.
Semipositivity then identifies the global maximum ranks of
\(\Theta_{P_c}+t\Theta_H\) and \(\Theta_{P_c}\) for each allowed choice
of weights. The prescribed-extension full mixed-mass argument makes all
\(L_j\) nef and converts both rank comparisons into the boxed equality.
The argument neither assumes orbit filling everywhere nor substitutes
zero Lelong numbers alone for the full mass comparison.
