Jan Philip Solovej, The ionization conjecture in Hartree-Fock theory.
Annals of Mathematics 158 (2003), 509-576.
DOI: 10.4007/annals.2003.158.509.
https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2003/158-2/p04
Locator: publisher Abstract, opened 2026-11-05.

"We prove the ionization conjecture within the Hartree-Fock theory of atoms.
More precisely, we prove that, if the nuclear charge is allowed to tend to
infinity, the maximal negative ionization charge and the ionization energy
of atoms nevertheless remain bounded. Moreover, we show that in Hartree-Fock
theory the radius of an atom (properly defined) is bounded independently of
its nuclear charge."
