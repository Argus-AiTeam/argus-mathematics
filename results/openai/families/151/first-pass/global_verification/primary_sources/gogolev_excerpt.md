# Prior finite-regularity statement

Andrey Gogolev, *A counterexample to the Shub entropy conjecture*,
internally dated October 11, 2026.
Primary URL: https://people.math.osu.edu/gogolyev.1/index_files/EC_example7.pdf.
The retrieved PDF is preserved alongside this excerpt; its exact
fingerprint is recorded in `provenance.json`.

Abstract, page 1, with mathematical typography normalized:

> For every finite r >= 1, we construct a non-invertible C^r self-map
> of S^1 x S^2 x S^2 x S^2 with zero topological entropy and eigenvalue
> 2 for f_* on the second homology group.

Section 4, "The signal preparation," equation (4.1), chooses
`R > ||DG||^r`. After the signal series (4.2), the text states:

> This series representation will be used to prove C^r regularity.

This is prior-work evidence, not an independent campaign proof of the
note's stronger theorem. Its finite-regularity and dimension claims
cannot be claimed as novel here.
