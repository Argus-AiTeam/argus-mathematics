Locators: Theorem 4.5, Definitions 2.4--2.6, paragraph after Definition 2.5.

Theorem 4.5:
"A vertex operator algebra, V, of CFT type is regular if and only if V is
C_2-cofinite and rational."

Definition 2.6:
"A vertex operator algebra is called regular if every weak module is a
direct sum of simple ordinary modules."

Reader's application check, not a quotation: the manuscript assumes CFT
type, rationality and C_2-cofiniteness. The paragraph following Definition
2.5 also supplies finiteness of simple types and ordinariness of simple
admissible modules. Finite-dimensional energy spaces bound the multiplicity
of each simple type at its lowest energy, so a grading-restricted module
is a finite sum. Mode adjoints make its decomposition orthogonal when the
module already has a positive invariant form. This does not unitarize
other modules.
