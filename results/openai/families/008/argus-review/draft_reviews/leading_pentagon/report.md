# ABC presentation and leading-pentagon transfer

**Engineer assessment: the ABC algebraic claim is supported; the full
count-filtered transfer has only partial independent support in the old
certificate. The source does give a proof of that transfer.**

## Exact scope and source

The old `L_ABC` and `L_LEADING_PENTAGON` refer to
`lem:abc-free` and `prop:leading-pentagon` in Section 3 of the pinned
September 23, 2026 manuscript, revision
`4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae`.

Work first in `F2<x,y>`, with associative substitutions

    A=x*x, C=[x,y]=x*y+y*x, B=y.

The claim is injectivity of the free-associative presentation in `A,C,B`,
and inclusion of every *ordinary* `x,y` Lie polynomial of weight greater
than one in the ordinary Lie subalgebra generated by these three elements.
Weights are `2,2,1`. For the leading-pentagon claim, the element is additionally
a leading symbol of the embedded reduction of a rational solution of
weight `n>2`; it is not an arbitrary polynomial in `A,C,B`.

## Decisive ABC argument

Order words by degree-lexicographic order, with `x>y`. The leading words of
the three substituted generators are `xx`, `xy`, and `y`. These form a
prefix code: a leading `x` consumes exactly two letters, and a leading `y`
consumes one. Thus distinct words in `A,C,B` have distinct leading image
words. The largest of them in a nonzero polynomial cannot cancel. This
proves injectivity of the associative substitution, rather than merely
injectivity in a finite tested degree.

Let `H` be the ordinary Lie subalgebra generated by `A,C,B` in this
associative algebra. In characteristic two,

    [x,A]=0, [x,B]=C, [x,C]=[A,B].

The derivation `ad_x` therefore preserves `H`; so does `ad_y`, because
`y=B` belongs to `H`. The degree-two generator brackets lie in `H`.
Induction on a bracket tree now puts every ordinary `x,y` Lie word of
weight at least two in `H`. Together with the ordinary free-Lie embedding,
this is the claimed presentation/inclusion, not a primitive-element
argument. In particular `A=x^2` being primitive in characteristic two
does not identify the entire primitive space with an ordinary free Lie
algebra on `x,y`.

## Leading relation and its evidence boundary

At `B`-count `r` and original weight `n=2m+r`, there are `m` letters of
type `A,C` and `r` of type `B`. The target symbolic length is consequently
`m+r`, not `n`. The source's leading-pentagon argument compares two
opposite ordering procedures, retaining count-preserving terms and
discarding lower-count terms. Independence is needed in the source
braid algebra. No independence of target arrangement monomials, and no
algebra homomorphism from source to target, should be silently added.

The source/reference sweep identifies a genuine ordering-and-action-table
argument in Section 3. The old certificate, however, independently expands
only the 21 **source-side** identities assessed in
[source_action_tables](../source_action_tables/report.md). It does not
separately certify the target table, the count annotations on exchanges,
or the universal ordered-word comparison. The compiled rational dimension
endpoint is not a proof of every such literal assertion.

The concrete obligation for separate certification is to account for every
source/target exchange used in both orderings, prove that the ordering
terminates without introducing a larger count, and match the surviving
count-r terms with the stated relation at symbolic length `m+r`. This is
the substantive missing independent check, not a request for more low-weight
examples. The manuscript presents that argument; no contradiction or absent
proof is asserted here.

## Disposition

`L_ABC` has a direct algebraic justification above. `L_LEADING_PENTAGON`
has a substantive source proof and relevant auxiliary evidence, but is
not separately certified by the old local computations. It remains
partially supported at that evidence level. This assessment does not
refute a manuscript statement or weaken the independently supported rational
endpoint. No originality, new theorem, or arbitrary modular-solution
strengthening is claimed. Native independent review remains required.
