## Exact claim and source version

Old `C_PRIOR29` versions 1 and 2 state the same finite-range comparison;
version 2 adds `A_NAEF_WILLWACHER29`. The source is Naef and Willwacher,
*Numerical computation of linearized KV and the Deligne-Drinfeld and
Broadhurst-Kreimer conjectures*, **arXiv:2508.08081v1, Theorem 5 and
Corollary 7**, with the intervening Conjecture 2 identifying the inclusions.

Theorem 5 supplies the displayed finite-weight linearized-KV dimensions
and spanning information. Corollary 7 states that the conjectured
inclusions are isomorphisms through and including weight 29 in their
stated weight range, starting at weight two. With the known inclusions
this supplies the cited Deligne-Drinfeld dimension comparison
`dim_Q W_n = dim_Q F_n` through weight 29. The separate elementary
weight-zero/one argument is not an output of that computation.

## Decisive inference and failed strengthening

The cited conclusion is explicitly bounded. It neither asserts the
universal rational upper bound nor permits extrapolating a table to all
weights. Even correct equality at every `n<=29` leaves infinitely many
degrees unaddressed. The old route `R_FINITE_ONLY` was therefore retired
with the explicit reason that a fixed finite range cannot settle the
universal quantifier. This is a failed *proof strategy*, not a refutation
of the finite-range theorem, and not a manuscript error.

No larger search or reproduction of the table is needed for this old-draft
review. The direct universal endpoint in the first unit improves on a
finite-range bound only in scope; it remains a reproduction of existing
upstream work, not a newly discovered theorem.
