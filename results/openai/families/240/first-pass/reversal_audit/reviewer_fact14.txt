Let K be an AEC and let theta be an infinite cardinal.

(1) [Shelah, Claim IV.1.12(1)] If K is categorical in a
lambda = lambda^(<theta), then for any M,N in K_(>=lambda),
M <= N implies M is an L_(infinity,theta)-elementary
substructure of N.

Footnote 3: Shelah assumes in addition that theta > LS(K),
but the proof shows that it is not necessary.

(2) [Shelah, Conclusion IV.2.12(1)] If theta > LS(K) and K
is categorical in a lambda > 2^(<theta) with
cf(lambda) >= theta, then whenever M,N are in K_(>=lambda),
M <= N implies M is an L_(infinity,theta)-elementary
substructure of N.
