# Scope and source propositions

The target is `found:reversal` in the staged September 24, 2026
*Eventual categoricity for abstract elementary classes*, Section 3, using
the order presentation in Section 2. The quantifiers include singular
categoricity cardinals and impose neither amalgamation nor cardinal
exponentiation assumptions. A verification of this section alone is not
a verification of the later calculus, geometry, or categoricity transfer.

The companion manuscript's `thm:main` assumes CH and asserts a countable
language AEC with LS number aleph_0, at least two models at
beth_(omega_2), and categoricity at every cardinal at least
beth_((2^(aleph_1))^+). Its obstruction concerns a prescribed threshold.
The eventual-categoricity manuscript asserts existence of an unspecified
uniform threshold. These statements have no immediate logical conflict.
Neither principal conclusion is assumed proved in this audit.

## What the literature supplies, and does not supply

Primary source opened: Boney and Vasey, *Categoricity and Infinitary
Logics*, arXiv:1508.03316v2,
<https://arxiv.org/html/1508.03316v2>, Section 1, Fact 1.4 and the
discussion following Claim 1.6 (the manuscript cites pp. 3-4).

Fact 1.4(1) states the following mathematical implication. For an AEC K
and infinite cardinal theta, categoricity at lambda=lambda^(<theta)
implies that M <=_K N, with both sizes at least lambda, entails
L_(infinity,theta)-elementarity.

Fact 1.4(2) instead assumes theta>LS(K), lambda>2^(<theta), and
cf(lambda)>=theta, in addition to categoricity at lambda, and has the
same elementarity conclusion.

Claim 1.5 states an unrestricted eventual version. The subsequent
discussion identifies a gap: a downward LS theorem for classical
L_(infinity,theta) satisfaction was used for model-theoretic forcing
without showing that the two satisfaction relations coincide. The
footnote also withdraws the authors' attempted repair: Lemma 2.20 of
versions dated before October 15, 2015 is false. This is a documented
failed route, not a counterexample to the unrestricted conclusion.

The two valid restricted results are recorded only as scope facts.
They will not be substituted for an unrestricted reversal proof.
The externally generated search summary was not used as evidence:
its description of the gap as hidden amalgamation differs from the
primary source's precise forcing-versus-satisfaction issue.

No claim of mathematical originality follows from this bounded lookup.
The staged manuscript itself already states the reversal theorem and
its proposed alignment/elimination mechanism. An independent check of
that mechanism must be described as verification, not discovery of an
unstated theorem.
