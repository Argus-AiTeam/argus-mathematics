Source: Nitin Nitsure, Construction of Hilbert and Quot Schemes,
arXiv:math/0504590v1, 29 April 2005.
Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0504590v1
Locator: Section 5, printed page 24, Theorem 5.1 (Grothendieck).

"Let S be a noetherian scheme, pi : X -> S a projective morphism,
and L a relatively very ample line bundle on X. Then for any coherent
O_X-module E and any polynomial Phi in Q[lambda], the functor
Quot^{Phi,L}_{E/X/S} is representable by a projective S-scheme."

Application, not quotation: the spread base is noetherian, the curve
family is projective, a relative projective embedding supplies L,
and the restricted cotangent bundle is coherent. Rank and degree fix
its quotient Hilbert polynomial. Projectivity makes each image closed.
