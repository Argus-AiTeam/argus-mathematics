## Lemma 2.3

The stated parameters are \(0<r_1<r_2<1\), \(\eta\in(0,1/2)\),
small \(\mathbf E_1\) depending on these parameters and the fixed
geometric data, and
\(\|V\|(\mathbf C_1)/\omega_m\le Q+3/4\).
The conclusion is an integer \(Q'\in\{0,\ldots,Q\}\) such that, for
every specified cylinder \(\mathbf C_r(x)\subset\mathbf C_{r_2}\),
\(r>r_1\),
\[
 {\|V\|(\mathbf C_r(x))\over\omega_m r^m}
       \in(Q'-\eta,Q'+\eta).
\]
This proposition concerns cylindrical mass, not an Lp height norm.

## Theorem 1.9

It assumes stationarity in \(\mathbf C_1\), a fixed \(r\in(0,1)\),
small \(\mathbf E_1\), and
\(\|V\|(\mathbf C_1)/\omega_m\le Q+1/2\).
Its first conclusion is support containment in the union of \(Q\)
height strips with radius
\[
 C(r)|\log\mathbf E_1|^{1-1/m}\mathbf E_1^{1/m}.
\]
The stronger single-strip square-root-excess conclusion additionally
assumes \(\Theta(0)=Q\).

## Section 1.4

The higher-integrability discussion is explicitly an integral of
\(|Df|^p\) for the Lipschitz approximation, equation (1.32), followed
by its asymptotic comparison in (1.33). It is not an integral of the
distance in height from a configuration. No negative conclusion about
AE's height moment is inferred from this gradient example.
