The section begins:
"Throughout this section M is supposed to be H^n-measurable with locally
finite H^n measure, and countably n-rectifiable".
It takes f locally Lipschitz on an open set U containing M, and specifies
target dimension Q >= n for the tangential Jacobian and area formula.

"We note also that if h is any non-negative H^n-measurable function on M,
then, by approximation of h by simple functions, 2.4 implies the more
general formula"

    integral_M h J_f^M dH^n
       = integral_{R^{n+k}} (integral_{f^{-1}(y)} h dH^0) dH^n(y).  (2.5)

The preceding (2.4) is the same formula for the characteristic function
of an H^n-measurable subset A of M.

For the audited application f is the normal projection, expressed in
base charts if necessary, the target dimension equals the varifold
dimension, and h includes the positive integer multiplicity. Applying
(2.4) to the zero-Jacobian subset gives a target-null image; it does not
declare the original mass of that subset zero.
