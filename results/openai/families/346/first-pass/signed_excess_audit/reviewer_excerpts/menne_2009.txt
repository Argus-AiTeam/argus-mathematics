Primary source inspected: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.3660v2
Ulrich Menne, A Sobolev Poincare type inequality for integral varifolds.
Locators: Definition 3.1; Corollary 3.8(1); Theorem 3.4(1);
Remark 3.6. In this source n denotes varifold dimension.
The formulas below transcribe the inspected primary statements.

Definition 3.1 defines H_q(mu,a,r,h,P) to include
    r^{-1-n/q} ||dist(.,spt P)||_{L^q(mu restricted to C(T,a,r,h))}
as its first summand, with a second summand comparing fiber counts.
H_q(mu,a,r,h,Q,T) is the infimum over Q-valued planes P parallel to T.
Thus this first summand uses full varifold mass, not just good fibers.

Corollary 3.8 assumes positive integers m,n,Q, 1 <= M < infinity,
0 < delta <= 1, a in R^{n+m}, 0 < r < infinity, T an n-plane,
1 <= p <= n, and an integral n-varifold mu in an open superset of
C(T,a,3r,3r), satisfying (H_p). It puts
    psi = ||delta mu||                  if p=1,
    psi = |H_mu|^p mu                  if p>1,
and assumes
    (Q-1+delta) omega_n r^n
       <= mu(C(T,a,r,r)) <= (Q+1-delta) omega_n r^n,
    mu(C(T,a,r,(1+delta)r) minus C(T,a,r,(1-delta)r))
       <= (1-delta) omega_n r^n,
    mu(C(T,a,3r,3r)) <= M omega_n r^n.

Part (1), for p<n and 1 <= q<n, states
    H_{nq/(n-q)}(mu,a,r,r,Q,T)
      <= Gamma [T_q(mu,a,3r,3r,T)
                + (r^{p-n} psi(C(T,a,3r,3r)))^{(n-q)/(q(n-p))}].
Gamma depends only on m,n,Q,M,delta,p,q.

Theorem 3.4 initially restricts to a set G with a quantitative
first-variation bound on every ambient ball of radius less than 2r.
Remark 3.6 warns that this restriction cannot generally be discarded.
For stationary mu the first variation vanishes and G contains the full
relevant support. In Corollary 3.8 with p=1 and q=2, n>2, psi=0,
so the resulting exponent is the strong critical 2n/(n-2).

This confirms stronger unweighted critical-height prior art under its
stated cylinder assumptions. It does not by itself establish their
applicability to every curved AE witness or novelty of prescribed-well
Gaussian localization.
