Theorem 16.4 (Circuit estimate) . There exists a constant c_{\mathrm{circuit}}>0 (independent of \mathbf{c}\in[1,2] ) such that for any r\in[4,\infty) and x\in\mathbb{R}^{2} , \mu_{\operatorname{Ball}_{2r}(x)}^{+}[\operatorname{Circuit}^{\omega^{-}}(\operatorname{Annulus}_{2r,r}(x))]\geq c_{\mathrm{circuit}}. (292) The same inequality remains true with + and - interchanged.
