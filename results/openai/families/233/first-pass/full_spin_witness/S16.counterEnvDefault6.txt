Corollary 16.6 (Circuit estimate) . For any r\in[4,\infty) and x\in\mathbb{R}^{2} , and for any even domain \mathcal{D} such that \partial\mathcal{D} surrounds \operatorname{Ball}_{2r}(x) , we have \mu_{\mathcal{D}}^{+}[\operatorname{Circuit}^{\omega^{-}}(\operatorname{Annulus}_{2r,r}(x))|\mathcal{E}]\geq c_{\mathrm{circuit}} (301) for any event \mathcal{E} that is measurable in terms of the even spins and edges which do not intersect \operatorname{Ball}_{2r}(x) . The same inequality remains true with \omega^{-} replaced by \omega^{+} .
