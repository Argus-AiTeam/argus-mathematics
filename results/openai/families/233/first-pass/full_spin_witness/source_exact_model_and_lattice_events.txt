Definition 15.3 (Lattice event) . A \circ -lattice event (or lattice event ) is an event of the form \{\omega|_{Q}=\zeta\}\cap\{\sigma_{\circ}|_{B}=\xi\}, (258) where B and Q contain vertices and edges of F_{\circ} respectively, and such that B contains all endpoints of edges in Q . Naturally, we require that \zeta\in\{0,1\}^{Q} and \xi\in\{\pm\}^{B} . Notice that if Q=B=\emptyset , then \mathcal{E} is just the entire sample space.
