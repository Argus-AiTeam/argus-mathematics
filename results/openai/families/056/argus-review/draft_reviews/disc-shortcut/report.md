# Retired simply connected disc shortcut

**Assessment: the shortcut is invalid; its exact permutation control is
supported.** This reviews old route CP_R_DISK_SHORTCUT and A07 Section 6.
It is a failed Argus attack, not a fourfold counterexample or a refutation
of an original manuscript.

## The inference being rejected

The curve bound counts distinct global primes centered at an entire compact
singular curve T. Counting irreducible local surface or divisor branches
after replacing its punctured open by a simply connected disc does not
give that global count: one global prime can have several restricted
components. Resolution/discrepancy arguments do not by themselves prevent
this splitting.

The mathematical control is a connected two-sheet cover with monodromy
generated by the transposition (0 1). Its action has one orbit {0,1}.
Restriction to a disc loses the generator and gives the identity action,
with two orbits {0} and {1}. Thus a local count of two cannot establish
two distinct global objects from this cover. The example is a concrete
failure of the needed covering-space inference, not an assertion that
every such cover arises from the specified terminal fourfold.

## Decisive evidence and repair

A29 `negative_controls` generates the two groups explicitly and checks
the orbit lists [[0,1]] and [[0],[1]]. The unchanged replay passed.
A04 records `restricted_image_surjective=false` and
`geometric_counterexample_claimed=false`; these qualifications are part
of the result, not incidental metadata.

The actual globalization proof instead chooses B with
pi_1(B)->pi_1(T0) surjective. A transitive finite permutation image stays
transitive under that restriction, so the Stein-factor cover stays
connected and the smooth global divisor restriction is one prime.
This is the precise repair. Merely choosing a larger disc, checking a
particular orbit example, or replacing global primes by field valuations
does not supply it.

The old ledger retired the route for this reason. The current review
records a separately reasoned retirement as RV_DISC_SHORTCUT, without
inheriting any old acceptance label. The control settles the invalid
proof step completely at its abstract level. It does not falsify b<=N_T,
the original manuscript, or any of its termination conclusions.
Independent whole-family acceptance is pending.
