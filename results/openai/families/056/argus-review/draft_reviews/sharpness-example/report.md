# Global quadric-cone product realizing b=N_T=1

**Assessment: supported as an explicit sharpness construction, with separate
prose geometric/topological reasoning and replayed finite algebra.**
It is a classical ordinary-double-point example, not a checked novelty
claim. This is A07 Section 5, which has no separate old CP claim node.

## Object and all required hypotheses

Let X={x0*x1-x2*x3=0} in P^4 and p=[0:0:0:0:1].
Put Y=X x P^1 and T={p} x P^1. The derivative ideal shows p is the only
singular point of X. The hypersurface is irreducible and Cohen--Macaulay;
its isolated singularity satisfies R1, hence X is normal by Serre's
criterion. Y is therefore a normal irreducible compact projective
fourfold, in particular compact Kahler, with Q-Cartier (indeed Cartier)
canonical sheaf and singular locus precisely the curve T.

Blow up p in X and take the product with P^1. The four affine charts near
p substitute each coordinate as an exceptional parameter times a direction.
After removing its square, the strict equation is a-b*c=0 or a*b-c=0.
Each chart has a unit partial derivative and is smooth. Away from p the
blowup is already an isomorphism. The exceptional quadric
Q={x0*x1-x2*x3=0} in P^3 is smooth, irreducible and isomorphic to
P^1 x P^1: its equation is the rank-one determinant equation and the
Segre map gives its two rulings.

Thus g=(Bl_p X) x P^1 -> Y is a projective resolution with smooth source,
isomorphism over Yreg and one smooth exceptional divisor Q x P^1.
In every blowup chart the singular ideal (x0,x1,x2,x3) is generated by
the exceptional parameter. This verifies the actual principalization and
full-support requirement, not just smoothness of a convenient resolution.
One exceptional component is SNC automatically.

## Discrepancy and terminality

The local ambient blowup is the blowup of a point in a smooth fourfold,
with relative canonical coefficient three. The hypersurface has
multiplicity two, so adjunction gives coefficient 3-2=1 on the
threefold resolution. Taking the product does not change it.
The exceptional prime therefore has **log discrepancy 2**, not coefficient
2. Any further exceptional place above the smooth resolution has log
discrepancy at least two; the composition adds the nonnegative exceptional
orders, strictly positively over this singular locus. Consequently every
exceptional place over Y has log discrepancy greater than one: Y is
ordinary terminal. For a place of log discrepancy two centered at T, the
strict positive-order comparison puts it on this resolution. There is only
one such global prime, so N_T=1.

## Cohomology is not just a matrix rank

The exceptional quadric has normal line bundle O_Q(-1,-1): it is the
tautological normal bundle for the blowup of the affine cone with
hyperplane bundle O_Q(1,1). A punctured local node has the homotopy type
of the circle bundle of this normal line. The Gysin sequence, with
H^1(Q,Q)=0 and H^2(Q,Q)=Q^2, therefore gives

    H^2(link,Q) = Q^2 / Q(1,1),  of dimension 1.

The first Gysin map is cup product with c_1(O(-1,-1)); in the ruling
basis its column is [-1,-1]. These normal-bundle, link and Gysin
identifications are the geometric/topological inputs. Computing the
column's rank alone would not prove them.

The singularity is the same product along every point of T. Thus
V=(R^2j_*Q)|T0 is constant of rank one. On any nonempty connected
finite-punctured T0 inside P^1, oriented-curve duality gives
dim H_c^2(T0,Q)=1. Hence b=1=N_T. This is a global compact fourfold
meeting the exact hypotheses, not a germ offered as a global example.

## Small resolution degree illustration

Locally write the node as det[[x0,x2],[x3,x1]]=0. Its incidence resolution
records the common line [s:t] of the two columns, with
t*x0=s*x3 and t*x2=s*x1. It is closed in P^1 x Xloc, hence projective
over Xloc, and is an isomorphism off the vertex. Its two charts are smooth;
over the vertex it has one P^1 and no exceptional divisor. Pulling back
O_P1(1), the **dual** tautological bundle, gives degree one on that curve.
This fixes the positive-degree convention in the draft's phrase
"tautological degree-one bundle" and explains the example matrix [1].
It is only an illustration of the general degree argument.
