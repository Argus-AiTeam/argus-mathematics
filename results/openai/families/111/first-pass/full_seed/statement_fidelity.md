# Statement fidelity: full-seed matroid selection

## Encoded statements

`FullSeedCertification.concrete_fixed_vector` quantifies over every natural
size `n`, every Mathlib matroid on `Fin n` whose ground set is all labels,
and every pointwise nonnegative real vector. It asserts independence of
the concrete algorithm's accepted set at **every prefix, seed, and
permutation**, and the factor `((2 : Real)^293)^(-1)` times the offline
optimum below the integral of `hiddenWorstReward`. The latter definition
is a finite minimum over **all** permutations, taken separately for each
seed, inside the integral. The rule is `completeHiddenRule M hE`, and the
law is `sourceSeedLaw n`; neither is an existential unspecified comparator.

`seed_dependent_orders` makes the information pattern explicit: for every
function from the entire finite seed to the arrival permutation, the
fixed-vector bound holds for the actual resulting reward. No seed
coordinate is hidden. There is no independence hypothesis on the order.
`exists_full_seed_minimizer` supplies an order function attaining the minimum
for every seed. `expected_minimum_iff_full_seed_orders` proves the converse
as well: on a finite seed space the expected-minimum inequality is equivalent
to the expected-reward inequality for every complete-seed order function.
Thus the formal minimum models exactly this adversary interface, not just
a convenient sufficient lower bound for a weaker collection of orders.

`concrete_one_sample` fixes the witness to
`sampleSimulation (completeHiddenRule M hE)` and the law to `sourceSeedLaw n`.
On any probability space it universally quantifies over the sample vector
`S`, online vector `V`, and complete seed `R`. It assumes their
measurability, almost-sure coordinate nonnegativity, mutual independence
of all sample/value coordinates, equality of the two marginal laws at
each label, independence of the whole sample/value pair from the seed,
the prescribed seed law, and integrability of the actual offline optimum.
For every measurable permutation on that same probability space, it
proves reward integrability and the stronger **2^-293** guarantee.
The order may depend on all samples, values, seeds, and any extra
information represented on the probability space. No other moment or
distributional hypothesis is added. `public_one_sample` also checks the
upstream existential statement with its published **2^-310** constant.

The rule's definition receives a single sample vector, never a distribution
or an additional draw. In `sampleSimulation`, only samples in the
seed-dependent mask are used; their online values are ignored and those
labels are rejected. `concrete_mask` computes that mask: `H union D union C`
in the main branch and `H` in the maximum branch. Its arguments contain
the seed and matroid but no weights.

The encoded seed has `4*n+2` possibly biased Boolean coordinates. Independent
unused-branch masks, or raw fair bits used to generate the sparse masks,
may be retained and exposed as well. Conditional on the chosen branch the
used coins have the same product law, while the reward as a function of
permutation ignores the unused coins. Hence its pointwise minimum is
unchanged. This correspondence is a projection of active information, not
an asserted bijection of complete seed encodings. In the one-sample
statement, arbitrary extra exposed information is also covered directly
by the unrestricted measurable order on the whole probability space.

## Independently added correspondence lemma

The manuscript greedily tests rank against previously **accepted** labels
of the same layer. The upstream implementation tests closure against all
previously **eligible** labels. These descriptions need an argument, not
just matching names.

`prior_closure_eq_accepted` proves equality of the two closures, with an
arbitrary base, arbitrary layer assignments, and arbitrary nonnegative
integer arrival times. Its proof uses the upstream prefix-spanning theorem
and proves both set inclusions. `singleton_rank_one_iff` identifies
conditional rank one with nonmembership in closure on a finite matroid.
Together `manuscript_greedy_decision_iff` proves that membership in the
upstream greedy set is exactly eligibility plus the manuscript's rank-one
test against previously accepted labels of the same layer. Actual
permutations have distinct times; the correspondence lemma is valid even
without that restriction.

## Source interpretation and dependencies

All imported OAI proof sources are pinned to openai/math commit
`4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae`. The pristine closure and its
import graph are in `upstream/`. `compat/` is generated by `build_compat.py`
for the assigned Lean 4.31.0 Mathlib workspace; translation of imports and
proof-language compatibility repairs are visible in that script.
Mathematical definitions and theorem hypotheses/conclusions are not
weakened. Compiler runs for the closure are recorded in `compat_build.json`.

The main proof depends on the actual upstream implementation, not the
`ComparatorChallenges` declarations. Its load-bearing interfaces are
the candidate-filter expectation, density optimizer and guarded paths,
adaptive reverse exposure, residual marked-pivot counting, common-generator
rank costs, independent test thinning, order-uniform weighted accounting,
rounding and branch mixing, and seed-dependent sample/value gluing.
The source correspondence and the precise declarations for these
interfaces are discussed in `report.md` and `safety_transfer_analysis.md`.

`Fin n` is a normalization of a finite labeled ground set, not a bound on
size or a representability assumption. An order-preserving enumeration
transports independent subsets, sums, closures, histories, masks, and
permutations to `Fin n`; the resulting rules transport back. That elementary
relabeling argument is stated here in prose, not separately formalized.
Loops, empty ground sets, rank zero, ties, zero coordinates, nonrepresentable
matroids, and arbitrary real-valued nonnegative coordinates remain allowed.

## What the compiler does not verify

Lean verifies the encoded propositions and their imported proof terms.
It does not parse the staged LaTeX, establish novelty or publishability,
approve this interpretation document, or constitute independent Reviewer
acceptance. The human-readable mapping of the manuscript's instructions
to the imported definitions, including the elementary relabeling argument,
is an explicit remaining responsibility of the independent Host Reviewer.
The finite Jacobian calculation and exact constant arithmetic are
supporting checks only; neither replaces the universal proof.
