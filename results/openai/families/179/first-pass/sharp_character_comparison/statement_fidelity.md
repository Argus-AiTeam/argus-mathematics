# Statement fidelity: sharp character comparison

The principal declaration is
`SharpCharacterComparison.sharp_character_comparison`
(unqualified name: `sharp_character_comparison`).

## Objects and quantifiers

The theorem quantifies over a type `A` with `CommRing`, `IsDomain`,
`CharZero` and `IsDiscreteValuationRing` instances, a natural prime `p`,
and the hypothesis that the actual maximal ideal of `A` is the ideal
generated by its natural-number cast `p`. These hypotheses include the
manuscript's unramified cyclotomic localization. They do not assume a
comparison conclusion, a factor-selection rule, or a valuation bound
on either input factor.

The natural parameters `r d s` are universal. In the mathematical report's
notation the character conductor exponent is **R = r+1**, the cyclic
exponent is **E = r+d+1**, and the scalar valuation is **s**. Thus every
pair `1 <= R <= E` occurs, by taking `r=R-1` and `d=E-R`. The explicit
inequality `hs : s <= d+1` is exactly `s+R <= E+1`. The natural type
includes `s=0`. There is no bounded-order restriction. `NeZero p` follows
from primality and is exposed only for the existing finite-cyclic APIs.

`F` and `K` are actual elements of
`AddMonoidAlgebra A (ZMod (p^(r+d+1)))`, the cyclic group ring.
`scalar` is the element supported at the identity, not a coefficient
sum or a matrix with the same diagonal. The equation `hFK` is the full
group-ring equality `F*K=scalar(p^s*b)`, with `b : Aˣ` an actual unit.
The two factors are independent; no star operation or self-duality is
assumed.

## Evaluation and conclusion

`root` is `AdjoinRoot.root` of the **actual**
`cyclotomic (p^(r+1)) A`; `root_pow` proves its required cyclic-order
power equation. The imported construction proves that this quotient
is the ramified cyclotomic domain and local ring over `A`. Its parameter
`RamifiedLocal.uniformizer` is `root-1`.
`value` is the group-ring algebra homomorphism evaluating the chosen
cyclic generator at that root. `value_scalar` identifies its coefficient
map, and `value_projection` proves compatibility with the actual cyclic
projection, not an arbitrary map.

`Compared` says there exists a unit `u` of this ramified ring with
`value(F)=u*algebraMap(augmentation(F))` and `u-1` in the ideal generated
by `root-1`. The latter is proved to be its actual maximal ideal by the
imported cyclotomic-local-ring construction. Thus it means **residue
one**, not just equal valuations or unspecified association.
The principal theorem asserts `Compared F` **and** `Compared K`.

The other printed declaration, `comparison_denominators`, quantifies
over an arbitrary cyclic order `n`, scalar exponent `s`, nonzero
coefficient `p`, a unit `b`, and the same full scalar product identity.
It proves that both augmentations are nonzero. Its hypotheses are
weaker than those of the principal theorem. Consequently the unit
identities in `Compared` really state the two fractions in Theorem 1,
not a vacuous relation with zero denominators. Evaluation products
are likewise nonzero by applying the evaluation homomorphism.

The quotient's distinguished root corresponds to any selected primitive
`p^R`-th root by the standard cyclotomic evaluation isomorphism: the
monic cyclotomic polynomial is its minimal polynomial over the
unramified coefficient fraction field. This identification is already
part of the imported cyclotomic construction, not a choice of one
bounded complex root.

## Proof content and limits

`projected_factor_at_valuation` separates the top cyclic exponent from
the scalar exponent and proves projected divisibility when the latter
is at least two. `comparison_step` proves scalar division and
comparison transport coefficientwise. `comparison_at_boundary` is a
genuine induction on the arbitrary conductor gap `d`, including
`d=0`. `sharp_character_comparison` covers all smaller scalar exponents
by padding one factor and cancelling a nonzero coefficient scalar.
At each stage either factor may be divided.

The file also has the helper declarations `root_pow`, `value_scalar`
and `value_projection`; none is an oracle.
The imported sources in `research/odd_square_audit/formal/` are the
already accepted Lean 4.31.0 compatibility snapshot. They are unchanged.
The printed axiom closures must be confined to `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`; `sorryAx` or any additional
unproved axiom is not acceptable.

This file formalizes **Theorem 1**, including its all-parameter range.
It does **not** formalize the Smith/cokernel classification, the
optimality family, its scalar-class annihilator, or any novelty claim.
Those are proved in `report.md` and separately replayed on finite exact
examples by `verify.py`; the independent Reviewer must check their

to `C_SHARP_COMPARISON`, not to those stronger claims.
