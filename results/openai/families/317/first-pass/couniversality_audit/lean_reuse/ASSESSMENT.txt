Explicit quantifier and semantic mapping
---------------------------------------
All OpenAI anchors below are relative to vendor/lean/OAI/CategoryTheory/Thomason/.

1. Small poset:
   {E : Type u} [PartialOrder E], with arbitrary universe u. The final
   couniversality theorems do NOT assume [Finite E].

2. Sieve:
   A : Set E and hA : IsLowerSet A. A itself is the subtype with its induced
   partial order; inc is the categorification of its actual subtype inclusion.

3. Right-adjoint retraction:
   r : E →o A and
     hadj : ∀ (a : A) (e : E), a.val ≤ e ↔ a ≤ r e.
   This is exactly inclusion ⊣ r, not the opposite adjunction. Retraction is
   automatic: for a : A, taking a and r a.val in hadj and applying
   antisymmetry yields r a.val = a. No additional unproved retraction
   hypothesis is needed. This calculation appears in
   AttachmentComparison.lean:286-289.

4. Strict categories and arbitrary strict functors:
   Definitions.lean:12-51 defines OmegaCategory by a Cell : Type u, source and
   target at every natural-number dimension, partial compositions, globular
   identities, units, associativity, interchange, and finite dimension for
   each individual cell. There is NO uniform bound on omega categories.
   Definitions.lean:62-70 defines strict functors by preservation of source,
   target, and every composition; :107-110 makes these the categorical Homs.
   There is no freeness, skeletal, cofibrancy, object-injectivity, or finite-
   carrier condition in these types.

   This is the single-sorted identity-collapsed encoding of strict higher
   categories, not a toy ordinary category. Its k-cells are the fixed points
   of source k; identities are their inclusions into higher fixed-point
   sets. Conversely a usual globular strict category gives this encoding by
   taking the union along identity inclusions. This representation comparison
   is explained here semantically, not supplied as an additional compiled
   equivalence to an external strict-category library.

5. Finite versus omega dimension:
   Definitions.lean:112:
     C.IsTruncated n := ∀ x : C.Cell, C.source n x = x.
   Definitions.lean:114:
     NCategory n := {C : OmegaCategory // C.IsTruncated n}.
   Its Homs remain ALL strict functors (:116-119).
   Thus finite n is literally n, with 1 ≤ n; it is NOT n+1.
   Omega is the separate OmegaCategory type, not a finite approximation and
   not an unspecified top element in NCategory.

6. Ordinary nerve N(E):
   CategoryTheory.nerve, imported from pinned Mathlib, has m-simplices
   ComposableArrows E m, i.e. functors Fin(m+1) → E. For the poset category,
   these are exactly weakly increasing strings. See
   vendor/mathlib_metadata/Mathlib/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Nerve.lean:
   13-18 and 35-40.

7. Actual c_n N(E), not E regarded as a 1-category:
   Definitions.lean:1880-1881 defines categorification as the left Kan
   extension of the raised oriental diagram along Yoneda.
   Definitions.lean:1962-1965 proves its Street-nerve adjunction.
   Definitions.lean:2143-2144 defines categorificationFinite n as
   categorification followed by the strict n-truncation reflector.
   Its adjunction is :2305-2307, not an assumed interface.
   CoherentGluing.lean:715-726 abbreviates
     posetObj n P = (categorificationFinite n).obj (nerve P)
   and inc n A = its map on the subtype-inclusion nerve.
   The omega theorem uses categorification.obj (nerve P) directly.
   These functors are NOT thomasonLeft or c_n Sd^2 N: the latter functors
   are separately defined in Definitions.lean:1891-1892,2154-2155.

8. Street nerve:
   Definitions.lean:1261-1262 defines orientals using the positive-table
   omega category of normalized augmented simplex chains. The simplex
   complex on m is built from Fin(m+1), not a truncated toy simplex.
   Definitions.lean:1902-1927 defines
     streetNerve C [m] = strict functors (orientals.obj [m]) C,
   with simplicial operators by precomposition and maps by postcomposition.
   streetNerveFinite n simply applies this nerve to C.val (:2165-2167).

9. Genuine weak equivalence, not an assumed WeakEquiv parameter:
   Definitions.lean:2326-2349 explicitly builds the induced maps on
   pi_0 and all positive homotopy groups of a continuous map:
     componentsMap bijective, and
     ∀ n : ℕ, ∀ x, groupMap (Fin(n+1)) f x bijective.
   Definitions.lean:2359-2360 defines simplicialWeakEquivalences by this
   condition on Mathlib's geometric realization SSet.toTop.map f.
   Therefore the couniversality conclusion is exactly weak homotopy
   equivalence of the geometric realization of the Street nerve of j.
   Mathlib's pinned TopAdj.lean describes and exhibits the geometric
   realization and its standard topological-simplex homeomorphisms.
   No predicate called WeakEquiv is a free parameter in the reused theorem.

10. Genuine pushout:
    The unqualified IsPushout resolves to CategoryTheory.IsPushout, not to
    a custom attachment relation. Its pinned Mathlib definition extends
    CommSq and requires Nonempty (IsColimit (PushoutCocone.mk ...)).
    See vendor/mathlib_metadata/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/
    Pullback/IsPullback/Defs.lean:78-82.
    OmegaCategory has actual colimits (Definitions.lean:1865-1866);
    NCategory n has actual colimits by reflection
    (Presentations.lean:469-470).
    Instantiating sq with IsPushout.of_hasPushout gives the literal
    j = pushout.inl a (inc n A), and likewise in omega dimension.
    The draft includes these explicit pushout.inl corollaries.

Precise reusable theorem route
-----------------------------
RankedFaithfulness.lean:665-672:
  GrayRecursive.IntervalHom.standardSimplexComparison_isIso :
    ∀ m : ℕ, IsIso (chainComparison
      (NormalizedChains.complex (ULift.{u} (Fin(m+3)))))

StandardComparison.lean:728-738:
  simplexComparison_isIso_of_standard
extends this to EVERY finite linear order P, including 0,1,2 elements.
The m+3 indexing therefore does not restrict the final dimensions or omit
empty/small simplices.

StandardComparison.lean:310-328:
  intervalExtensionOfSimplexIsIso
constructs the concrete RightIntervalExtension, filling tensor,
right-adjoint existence, endpoints, projections, simplex isomorphisms,
and globe isomorphisms from proved constructions.

Let T be this constructed extension. Choose R := T.tensor.rightAdjoint
using T.rightAdjoint and adj := Adjunction.ofIsLeftAdjoint T.tensor.
Then apply:

  Couniversality.poset_couniversal T R adj n hn A hA r hadj sq
    (AttachmentComparison.lean:329-342)

  Couniversality.omega_poset_couniversal T A hA r hadj sq
    (AttachmentComparison.lean:344-356).

Their types quantify over arbitrary E, arbitrary X,Y, and arbitrary a,j,v
with the actual pushout square. The two exact types, and their literal
pushout.inl specializations, are written in CouniversalityWrapper.lean.txt.

This route does not merely leave SimplexDescent n as a new hypothesis.

also enables the public omega theorem. Neither Thomason.main nor the
parallel Transfer/Main endpoint is needed. The parallel construction is
therefore not part of this dependency closure or the proposed export.
