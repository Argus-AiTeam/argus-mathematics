Source: Crandall, Ishii, Lions, User's guide to viscosity solutions of
second order partial differential equations, arxiv:math/9207212v1,
Theorem 3.2, printed page 17. Downloaded primary PDF: CIL1992.pdf.
The following is an ASCII mathematical transcription of the statement.

Let O_i be a locally compact subset of R^{N_i} for i=1,...,k,
O=O_1 x ... x O_k, u_i in USC(O_i), and phi be twice continuously
differentiable in a neighborhood of O. Set
w(x)=u_1(x_1)+...+u_k(x_k) for x in O, and suppose xhat in O is a
local maximum of w-phi relative to O. Then for each epsilon>0 there
exists X_i in S(N_i) such that

  (D_{x_i} phi(xhat), X_i) in closed J^{2,+}_{O_i} u_i(xhat_i),

and the block diagonal matrix with entries X_i satisfies

  -(1/epsilon + ||A||) I <= diag(X_1,...,X_k) <= A + epsilon A^2,

where A=D^2 phi(xhat), N=N_1+...+N_k.

Scope comparison: small product balls around the maximizing triple
are locally compact subsets of Euclidean space, all three functions
U_i,U_i,-2U_i are continuous, and their product can be chosen to avoid
the singular set x=y. Thus the original theorem's C2-neighborhood
hypothesis is met, not merely C2 differentiability at one point.
No parabolic time-derivative hypothesis occurs in this theorem.
