\begin{abstract}
In connection with Kirby Problem~2.27 on embeddings and homomorphisms between braid groups and right-angled Artin groups (RAAGs), we classify all solutions in the projection fiber over the pure-braid triple $(A_{12}, 1, A_{12})$: combining an invertible generator transformation to a path RAAG, faithful Artin action, semidirect-product decomposition, and the Crisp--Wiest square-root uniqueness theorem in pure braid groups, every solution in the fiber has the exact form $(u, v, u\alpha(v))$ with $u\alpha(v)=vu$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Main Fiber Classification Theorem}
\begin{theorem}
In the semidirect-product fiber over the pure-braid projection triple $(A_{12},1,A_{12})$, an invertible change of generators identifies the fiber kernel with a path right-angled Artin group, and the Crisp--Wiest unique-root property forces every commuting/braid-compatible lift to take the form $(u, v, u\alpha(v))$ satisfying the twisted commutation relation $u\alpha(v)=vu$.
\end{theorem}
