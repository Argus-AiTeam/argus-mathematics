# Public result classification policy

Effective 2026-08-31, mathematical correctness and originality are displayed
as separate claims.

## Original results

A result may appear under **Original mathematical results** when:

1. its mathematical claim has passed the applicable internal review;
2. the project produced the proof, construction, or newly identified scope
   observation rather than merely restating a located source; and
3. no equivalent prior result is currently known to the project; and
4. the claimed contribution is not merely a parameter choice, worked example,
   certificate, direct corollary, or computational instantiation of a located
   theorem whose hypotheses already imply the advertised conclusion.

`No prior result found` or `novelty unverified` may support only a provisional
project-original label after a claim-level dependency audit has checked the
strongest located theorems and shown that none already implies the result.
Absence of the exact numerical example from the literature is not enough when
the mathematical conclusion is a direct application of prior work. These
checks are not certificates of worldwide priority, so that limitation must
remain explicit on the result page.

Before publication, the review record must separately answer:

1. Is the mathematical argument correct?
2. Does it answer the exact source question rather than a stronger, weaker, or
   literal variant?
3. Which cited theorem supplies each decisive implication?
4. After removing those imported implications, what theorem-level novelty
   remains?
5. Is the remaining contribution more than an explicit example or replay?

## Corrections and status notes

The following all belong under **Errata, scope corrections, and known-result
reconstructions**:

- source indexing or statement defects;
- counterexamples only to a literal overbroad formulation;
- applications or reconstructions of existing theorems;
- results whose negative status was already known;
- direct reconstructions of an answer already recorded by an official source.

Under this policy, the twelve public result pages existing after publication
of Results 2813, 4600048, and 2307015 on 2026-08-31 are displayed as four
project-original results and eight corrections, interpretation-limited
results, or known-result reconstructions. Result 2813 is an explicit worked application of Yi Liu's
arXiv:2409.14504, Lemma 3.2, and is not counted as an original mathematical
result. Worldwide priority remains unclaimed where it has not been
established.

Result 2307015 is displayed outside the original-results category. Its exact
Jordan-domain calculation and finite source-vocabulary audit are independently
reviewed, but the printed source does not explicitly name the translated-cusp
contact point. Until an external source or authorial clarification fixes that
interpretation and a separate novelty audit is complete, it must remain
labelled interpretation-limited and must not be promoted as an unconditional
or original resolution.
