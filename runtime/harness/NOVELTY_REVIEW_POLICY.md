# Strict novelty review policy

Mathematical correctness, claim alignment, significance, and novelty are four
separate review gates. Passing one never implies passing another.

## Default classification

Every project-produced proof, construction, counterexample, or scope
observation starts as `novelty_pending`. Failure to locate prior art is not
evidence of originality.

## Promotion to confirmed original

A result may be labelled `confirmed_original` only when a Reviewer independent
of the producing and mathematical-review roles records
`novelty_status=independent_novelty_review_passed` and supplies all of:

1. the exact claim and its strongest defensible scope;
2. a search record covering terminology variants and notation-equivalent forms;
3. direct comparison with the closest located prior results;
4. primary-source or full-text evidence for every exclusion;
5. an explicit priority cutoff date and unresolved-search boundary;
6. a signed Reviewer decision that no located source contains the same result.

Expert or editorial confirmation is required when the claim depends on a
specialist interpretation of an old source or problem statement.

## Public wording

Before promotion, public pages must say `project-produced candidate; novelty
not independently verified`. They must not say original, first, new theorem,
world first, breakthrough, or an equivalent phrase without the pending
qualifier. A later prior-art match immediately downgrades the result to known
result, reconstruction, correction, or interpretation-limited status.
