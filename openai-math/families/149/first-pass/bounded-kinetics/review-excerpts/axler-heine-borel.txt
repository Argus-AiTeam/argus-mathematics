Theorem 2.12, p.19, Heine-Borel Theorem.

"Every open cover of a closed bounded subset of R has a finite subcover."

This is a scalar statement. The report derives the finite-dimensional
compactness it needs by successive coordinate subsequences, and obtains
extrema by continuous images of compact sets. No stronger dimensional
statement is attributed directly to this numbered theorem.
