Theorem 89: "Let F: [a,b] -> R be absolutely continuous. Then
integral_[a,b] F'(x) dx = F(b)-F(a)."

Exercise 87: "In particular (by Exercise 75), absolutely continuous
functions are differentiable almost everywhere."

Exercise 87: "If f: R -> R is absolutely integrable, show that the
indefinite integral F(x) := integral_[-infinity,x] f(y) dy is absolutely
continuous, and that F is differentiable almost everywhere with
F'(x)=f(x) for almost every x."

Theorem 15 additionally states the almost-everywhere derivative formula
for an absolutely integrable complex-valued f on R.
For the local integral equation, extend the bounded integrand on the
compact time interval by zero; apply the real statements componentwise.
