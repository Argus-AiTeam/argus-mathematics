Gerald Teschl, Ordinary Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems.
https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-ode/ode.pdf
Chapter 2, Theorem 2.1, p.35. Mathematical notation transcribed into ASCII.

"Let C be a (nonempty) closed subset of a Banach space X and let
K : C -> C be a contraction, then K has a unique fixed point x in C".

The contraction constant is in [0,1).
The report applies this to the closed sup-norm ball of continuous paths.
Measurability and boundedness of the polynomial integrand establish the
integral operator independently; no continuous-time Picard theorem is used.
