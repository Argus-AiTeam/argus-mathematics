Heard, arXiv:1501.07759v2, proof of Theorem 5.6, printed page 13:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.07759v2#page=13
The setting is n=p-1, p odd, and the maximal finite extended stabilizer
subgroup whose order is divisible by p. The passage identifies alpha and
beta with the images of the named stable alpha_1 and beta_1 under the
unit S^0 -> E_n^{hG}. Thus it concerns an actual unit, not just an
arbitrarily named cohomology generator.

Heard, arXiv:1501.07759v2, Lemma 5.8, printed page 14:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.07759v2#page=14
The lemma is explicitly about the ordinary spectral sequence
H^*(G,(E_n)_*) => pi_*(E_n^{hG}), in positive cohomological degrees.
Its even-filtration formula is
E_infinity^{even,*} = F_p[Delta^{+/-p}][beta]/(beta^{n^2+1}).
Consequently beta^16 is nonzero at p=5,n=4; beta^17 is zero in this
associated graded. The p=5 specialization is a deduction, not an
additional quotation. No actual homotopy vanishing of beta_1^17 follows.

Heard--Mathew--Stojanoska, arXiv:1511.08064v1, Proposition 2.7 and
following discussion, printed pages 8-9:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.08064v1#page=8
For n=p-1 and a maximal finite extended subgroup containing p-torsion,
the positive-filtration cohomology has alpha, beta, Delta in bidegrees
(1,2n), (2,2pn), (0,2pn^2). The comparison
Ext_BPBP(BP_*,BP_*) -> H^*(G_extended,E_*) -> H^*(G_finite,E_*)
sends alpha_1 and beta_1 to alpha and beta up to nonzero scalars;
the following discussion states their permanence.

The reader did not find an explicit assertion of the sphere element
beta_1 having order p in these passages. That assertion is not used as
a substituted citation: the upper proof instead uses Serre's
positive-stable-stem torsion theorem.
