# Statement fidelity: exact-transitivity algebraic core

The source is `Main.lean`, namespace `ExactTransitivity`. Its matrix theorems
quantify over an arbitrary field K, with no characteristic restriction. Its
additive-amplification theorems quantify over an arbitrary division ring D
and an arbitrary target type B. Thus characteristic two and noncommutativity
in the D-valued arguments are not silently excluded.

`separator_lower_left` gives the exact lower-left entry of
[[diag(x,1),g],[diag(y,1),g]] for g = [[a,b],[c,d]]. The hypotheses are x != 0,
y != 0, and ad-bc != 0. `phaseCommInv` is the inverse commutator word, not an
independently chosen matrix. The explicit right side is the rational formula
used to select a nonzero entry by putting y = x^2. The compiler does not check
the subsequent selection of a phase outside finitely many roots.

`triangular_phase_comm` identifies [diag(x,1),g] with an actual upper elementary
matrix when c = 0 and a,d,x are nonzero. `opposite_commutator` identifies the
lower-left entry of [U(x),L(y)] with x*y^2. `commutator_in_intersection` proves
that a commutator of members of two normal subgroups lies in their
intersection. Normality is expressed as conjugation closure in the ambient
group, not as Zariski normality or closure of a dense image.

`four_shear_diagonal` is the four-factor identity with u = 1+s*t != 0.
`normalizing_matrix` computes
h^-1 g^-1 h g, where r = a/c and
h = U((1-u^2)r) diag(u,u^-1).
The result is an upper triangular determinant-one matrix with diagonal
(u^-2,u^2). The hypotheses c != 0, u != 0, and ad-bc != 0 are explicit.

`normal_extract` is a subgroup-membership theorem, not just a matrix identity.
H and P are subgroups of the group of units of 2-by-2 matrices. It assumes
that P is closed under conjugation by H, p belongs to P, the displayed
entries of p have nonzero determinant and lower-left entry, and the two
actual matrices h and U(x) belong to H. It proves that the actual unit
U((u^-4-1)x) belongs to P. `extracted_shear_nonidentity` proves this unit is
not the identity when u^4 != 1 and x != 0. No density hypothesis is converted
to membership by a tactic.

`exact_additive_amplification` assumes that the abstract additive subgroup
generated by the values of a subgroup V of D^times is the whole additive
group of D. The use of `AddSubgroup.closure` here means algebraic generated
subgroup, **not topological closure**: D has no topology in this section.
An invariant function f:D->B, invariant under left V-multiplication and
one nonzero additive increment at every argument, is proved constant.

`pair_invariant_constant` assumes independent left V-invariance on D^2,
the same exact additive-generation hypothesis, a nonzero upper increment
for every fixed c != 0, and a nonzero lower increment for every fixed
a != 0. Each increment is a transformation valid at every value of the
other coordinate. The conclusion is f(a,c)=f(1,1) for every nonzero pair.
Applied to orbit classes, this is exact transitivity. Applied to the
manuscripts' F_d, it is the constancy step. This formal statement does not
assume that an increment at one point extends to a global transformation.

Not formalized here: construction and admissibility of the triangular
boost words; finite-root avoidance on E^1; the global-field phase-ring
lemma and congruence units; weak approximation, the local Brauer and
Hermitian-form inputs, or the exact arithmetic proof Z U(k)=D; the
definition/pair-invariance of F_d or the remaining Margulis--Platonov
argument. Those are separate propositions in `report.md` and the ledger,
with symbolic identities replayed by `verify.py`. A successful compiler
run certifies the declarations above, not the complete manuscripts,
the report's mathematical reading, or novelty.
