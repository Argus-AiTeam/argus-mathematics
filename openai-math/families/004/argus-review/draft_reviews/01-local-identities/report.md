# Local polynomial identities

## Claim, source, and attribution

This unit covers the Cyclic section of `lean/Main.lean` in namespace
`IntegralTwoSwitch`, the
universal-identity part of `verify.py`, and the corresponding parts of the old
report's "Local identities and finite precision." Source paths in this report
are relative to the read-only `9000004` workspace unless stated otherwise.
The source application is the October 7, 2026 companion,
revision `4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae`,
`ring-determinants.tex`, `rd:derivative-identity`, `rd:exact-frobenius`,
and `rd:half-factor`. These are reproductions of elementary algebra, not
verified novel results or an independent proof of the companion.

For every commutative ring R, s in R, and natural number n, put
D_n=sum_{0<=i<n} i*s^i and N_n=sum_{0<=i<n} s^i. The exact claim is

    (s-1)D_n + N_n - n = (n-1)(s^n-1).

Under s^n=1 this gives `(s-1)D_n=n-N_n`. Also, for f,a,l in R
with `f*f-a*f+l=0`,

    (f-a)(a*f-(l+1)) = a-(l+1)*f.

For integers b,k, setting q=1+2*b*k gives
`1-q=b*(-2*k)` and `q*(q-1)/2=b*k*q`. Setting l=2*b*k-1 gives
`l*(l+1)/2=b*k*l`. In arithmetic use b=2^a and the appropriate
Frobenius/prime congruences; arbitrary formal f,a,l are not automatically
those arithmetic operators.

## Decisive argument and statement fidelity

The cyclic identity is proved by induction for arbitrary n, including n=0,
not by trying a range of cyclic orders. Replacing D_n by D_n+n*s^n and
N_n by N_n+s^n makes the induction defect vanish identically.
The Frobenius defect is exactly
`a*(f*f-a*f+l)`, with no division by 2. Thus the later reduction a=0
at retained precision gives s=F*f with the displayed orientation.
Each integer factorization above follows by substitution; its divisibility
conclusion is for the stated modulus, not for an unspecified precision.

Lean's `derivative_telescoping`, `cyclic_derivative_identity`, and
`exact_frobenius` have exactly these ring quantifiers and hypotheses.
Applying the first to a commutative cyclic group algebra is faithful.
Applying the second to a Frobenius endomorphism is faithful inside the
commutative subalgebra generated by that one endomorphism and the scalars;
it is not a theorem about arbitrary noncommuting endomorphisms.
The integer Fox/half-factor factorizations are checked by Python, not
additional Lean declarations. `two_by_two_volume` belongs to unit 06.

The successful reduced CITS-ALG v1 certificate already covers the three
Lean identities. The successful full v5 certificate covers the same
statements, not a stronger arithmetic theorem. Failed full versions v2-v4
do not refute them. Fresh compilation of the unchanged full source is
shared with unit 07; its source/fidelity documents are not rewritten here.

integral Selmer duality. In particular, vanishing of the two displayed

manuscript, no novelty, and no arithmetic counterexample follows here.
