to universal linear clearing. Unit 06 gives the determinant
comparison **conditional on the actual integral diagrams**. Unit 07
supplies one fully coherent algebraic family for arbitrary
nonprimitive parameter and retained strict torsion. Thus the
structural algebraic parts have substantive support.

Units 03-05 do not establish all arithmetic inputs needed to
instantiate that structure. The scalar proposition's exact
group-level/CM scope was not obtained; uniform Heegner/local
descent inputs and actual target diagrams remain obligations.
Consequently neither an unconditional CITS-UNIFORM certification
nor the existence claim CITS-REFUTE has been established.

Two retired approaches remain retired, with their reasons preserved
in the active ledger. RITS-SCALAR-ONLY fails because operator
identities alone do not control descent ambiguity or integral
dualities. RITS-RATIONAL-ONLY fails because rational cohomology
forgets the common torsion determinant and does not provide
cochain maps/null-homotopies. The explicit coordinates +/-p^(-2)
make the latter loss visible. Retiring these arguments is not a
refutation of the original manuscript.

The four rejected checker mutations are falsification controls;
they violate specified compatibility data. They are not four
elliptic-curve counterexamples. Likewise the graph's universal
linear-clearing obstruction concerns an Argus strengthening,
not a source assertion that the clearing must be linear.
