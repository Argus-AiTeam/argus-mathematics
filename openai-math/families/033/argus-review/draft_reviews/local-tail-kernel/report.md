# The local common kernel of all Higgs tails

**Assessment:** the first two tails determine every weighted tail kernel
under the stated local Hodge hypotheses, without a rank or tail-length
bound. The exact algebraic component replays successfully. It is not,
by itself, a certificate for global geometry.

## Exact statements and attribution

`C_TAIL_KERNEL` v1 concerns **every** polarized complex pure variation on
any complex manifold and **every holomorphic line** \(\ell\) in its whole
highest Hodge bundle. Work where all subspaces generated by the Higgs
contractions have constant rank. Let
\[
 G_i=\operatorname{span}\{\theta(w_1)\cdots\theta(w_i)\ell\},\quad
 G_0=\ell,\quad U_j=\bigoplus_{i\ge j}G_i,\quad L_j=-\det U_j.
\]
There are finitely many nonzero grades, but no uniform bound is imposed.
With the induced Hodge metrics, for \(c_0,c_1>0,\ c_j\ge0\) for \(j\ge2\),
\[
 \ker(\Theta_{L_0}+\Theta_{L_1})
  =\bigcap_j\ker\Theta_{L_j}
  =\ker\left(\sum_jc_j\Theta_{L_j}\right).
\]
Algebraicity, coefficient irreducibility and monodromy assumptions are
**not** required for this local assertion.
The historical ledger groups it under `CTX_HIGGS_TAIL`, but its natural
statement expressly has these broader local quantifiers; that grouping
does not export the hypothesis-free local claim to the global comparison.

`C_TAIL_LOCAL` v1 is the preliminary consequence: null curvature of
\(L_0+L_1\) gives \(A^*G_1=0,\ A\ell=0\), and zero projective flat
derivative of \(\ell\), where \(A=\theta(v)\). In the marked setting,
pointwise compatible compact marks also have zero derivative. That last
conclusion needs the compatibility hypothesis; it is not an assertion
about arbitrary independently chosen marks.

Both statements come from Section 2 of the old Argus report
(`engineer:copilot`), not separate draft copies. The common-kernel
assertion genuinely strengthens the preliminary two-tail observation.
It is the local component of the stronger numerical theorem, not an
Iitaka-dimension theorem. Its publication-level novelty was not established.

## Curvature and the decisive deduction

Higgs integrability makes every \(U_j\) Higgs invariant. For the orthogonal
projection \(Q_j\), grading-preserving Chern connection \(\mathcal D\),
and \(B_j=(1-Q_j)\mathcal D'_v|_{U_j}\), subbundle curvature gives
\[
 \Theta_{L_j}(v,\bar v)=
  \|(1-Q_j)A^*|_{U_j}\|_{\rm HS}^2+\|B_j\|_{\rm HS}^2.
\]
The sign is important: for the invariant block matrix
\(A=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\0&c\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), the
trace of \(A^*A-AA^*\) on \(U_j\) is \(-\|b\|^2\); the subbundle
term is \(-\|B_j\|^2\). Negating the determinant makes both terms positive.

Nullity of the first two curvatures kills each norm. Since
\(A^*G_1\) is in the top grade and is orthogonal to \(U_1\), it is zero.
As \(A\ell\subset G_1\), positivity gives \(A\ell=0\).
The condition \(B_0=0\), together with preservation of grades, puts
\(\mathcal D'_v\ell\) in \(\ell\); hence
\(\nabla^{1,0}_v\ell=(\mathcal D'_v+A)\ell\) has zero projective derivative.

All contractions commute with \(A\). Induction on **arbitrary Higgs words**
therefore gives \(AU_0=0\). The \(j=0\) null condition says
\(A^*U_0\subset U_0\), while \(AU_0=0\) says this image is orthogonal
to \(U_0\). Thus \(A^*U_0=0\).
Also \(\mathcal D'_vU_0\subset U_0\); isolating each Hodge grade gives
\(\mathcal D'_vG_i\subset G_i\). Consequently every \(B_j\) vanishes,
and so does every tail curvature. The reverse and weighted implications
follow from semipositivity. Empty tails and \(G_1=0\) cause no exception.

## What actually replayed

`replay.json` records the successful execution of the unchanged old
`verify.py`, with the Argus legacy interpreter, Python 3.13.5, standard
library only, exit 0, no timeout and empty stderr. The checker reads the
old certificate and saved Jacobian request/output without writing there.

It verifies eight exact ideal-membership identities in a free associative
algebra: line annihilation, \(A^*G_1=0\), flat-line derivative, the
orbit-to-universal-commutator implication, an arbitrary-word induction
step, annihilation of \(U_0\), annihilation of each grade, and Chern
preservation of each grade. The induction hypothesis is local to the
step; the adjoint relation is derived after the base and step, not added
as an unsupported geometric premise. The block-rank certificate checks
both inverse products and the reduction of
\((p,b,cp)^{\mathsf T}\) to \((p,b,0)^{\mathsf T}\).
All 10 negative controls are rejected.

These are universal exact identities, **not** tests at a bounded matrix
size. The separate sl4 example is bounded and serves a different purpose.
The interpreter checks polynomial equalities using rational arithmetic;
it is not a Lean kernel. One must still verify that the orthogonal
projections, derivative operators and geometric subbundles satisfy the
encoded relations. The curvature/grading argument above supplies that
interpretation; the program does not derive Hodge theory from nothing.
