Proposition 1.3: "The category K-tilde is additive, the functor
iota : K -> K-tilde is additive and K-tilde is idempotent complete.
Moreover, the functor iota induces an equivalence
Hom_add(K-tilde, L) -> Hom_add(K, L)
for each idempotent complete additive category L, where Hom_add
denotes the (large) category of additive functors."

Definition 1.10: "Let K be a triangulated category." It defines a
triangle Delta in K-tilde "to be exact when it is a direct factor
of an exact triangle of K. That is when there is an exact triangle
Delta' of K and triangle maps s : Delta -> Delta' and
r : Delta' -> Delta with r o s = 1_Delta. Equivalently, when there
is a triangle Delta'' in K-tilde such that Delta direct_sum Delta''
is isomorphic to an exact triangle in K."

Theorem 1.12: "With the collection of exact triangles given by 1.10,
K-tilde is a triangulated category."
