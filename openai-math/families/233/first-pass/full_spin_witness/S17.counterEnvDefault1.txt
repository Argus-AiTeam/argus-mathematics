Theorem 17.1 (Full-plane limit) . There exists a unique probability measure \mu_{\mathbb{Z}^{2}} on \Omega_{\mathbb{Z}^{2}} such that for any random variable X measurable in terms of finitely many spins and edges, \lim_{\mathcal{D}\nearrow\mathbb{Z}^{2}}\mu_{\mathcal{D}}^{+}[X]=\lim_{\mathcal{D}\nearrow\mathbb{Z}^{2}}\mu_{\mathcal{D}}^{-}[X]=\mu_{\mathbb{Z}^{2}}[X]. (306) Moreover, \mu_{\mathbb{Z}^{2}} satisfies the following properties: (i) \mathbb{R}^{2}\setminus\omega contains \mu_{\mathbb{Z}^{2}} -almost surely no unbounded connected components. (ii) Conditional spin flip property for \sigma^{\bullet} (analogous to Lemma 14.7 ), (iii) Markov property (analogous to Lemma 14.12 ), (iv) Markov property for polar domains (analogous to Lemma 14.13 ), (v) FKG inequality (analogous to Proposition 15.4 ), (vi) Circuit estimate (analogous to Corollary 16.6 ).
