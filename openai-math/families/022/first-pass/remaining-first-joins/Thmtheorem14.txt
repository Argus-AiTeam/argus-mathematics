Theorem 14 (The case of rational $\gamma$ ) . Let $\psi:\mathbb{N}\to{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}$ be an arbitrary function and $\gamma=A/B\in\mathbb{Q}$ , where $A,B\in\mathbb{Z}$ , $B>0$ and $(A,B)=1$ . Then for almost every $x\in[0,1]$ the number of integer pairs $(a,q)$ satisfying $\lvert qx-\gamma-a\rvert\leq\psi(q)\hskip 23.49976pt\text{and}\hskip 23.49976pt(A+aB,q)=1$ (78) is infinite if ( 52 ) holds, and finite otherwise. Consequently, the weak inhomogeneous Duffin-Schaeffer conjecture holds for all $\gamma\in{\mathbb{Q}}$ . That is, for all $\gamma\in{\mathbb{Q}}$ , and for all $\psi:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}$ satisfying ( 52 ), for almost every $x\in[0,1]$ inequality ( 76 ) holds for infinitely many $q\in{\mathbb{N}}$ .
