Primary source inspected: staged September 23, 2026 AE manuscript,
build/sections/introduction.tex, definition of regularity and thm:main.

"A point p in spt(mu) intersect U is regular if there are a neighborhood
W of p, a smooth embedded minimal m-submanifold Sigma contained in W, and
an integer q >= 1 such that the restriction of the varifold to W is
q|Sigma|. The remaining support points form Sing V."

"Every stationary integral m-varifold in an open subset of R^{m+n}
satisfies
    H^m(Sing V) = 0.
Here m,n >= 1 are arbitrary. No stability, minimizing, orientability,
density upper bound, or codimension-one hypothesis is required."

The preceding source definition assumes countable m-rectifiability,
positive locally integrable integer multiplicity, and vanishing first
variation against every compactly supported C^1 ambient vector field.
This attribution confirms what is printed, not the truth of the main
regularity theorem.
