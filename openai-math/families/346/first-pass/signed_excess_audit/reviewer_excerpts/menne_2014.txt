Primary source inspected: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.3287v1
Ulrich Menne, Weakly differentiable functions on varifolds.
Locators: hypotheses 6.1; Theorem 10.7(3); Example 8.7.
The formulas below transcribe the inspected primary statements.

Hypotheses 6.1 assume positive integer dimensions m<=n, 1<=p<=m,
U open in R^n, V an m-varifold, ||delta V|| a Radon measure, and
Theta^m(||V||,x)>=1 for ||V|| almost every x.
For p>1 they additionally assume h(V,.) in L^p_loc and
    delta V(theta) = -integral h(V,x) dot theta(x) d||V||(x).
For p=1, psi=||delta V||; for p>1,
    psi(A)=integral_A |h(V,x)|^p d||V||(x).

Theorem 10.7 fixes 1<=M<infinity and gives Gamma>0. In addition to
6.1 it assumes a finite dimensional normed space Y,
sup{dim Y,n}<=M, f in T(V,Y), 1<=Q<=M, a positive integer N, r>0,
    ||V||(U) <= (Q-M^{-1})(N+1) alpha(m) r^m,
    ||V||{x:Theta^m(||V||,x)<Q} <= Gamma^{-1} r^m.
It defines
    A={x:U(x,r) subset U},
    f_Upsilon(x)=dist(f(x),Upsilon).

Part (3) assumes 1<=q<m=p and psi(U)<=Gamma^{-1}.
It concludes that some Upsilon subset Y with 1<=card Upsilon<=N obeys
    ||f_Upsilon||_{L^{mq/(m-q)}(||V|| restricted to A)}
       <= Gamma N^{1/q-1/m}(m-q)^{-1}
                       ||VDf||_{L^q(||V||)}.

Example 8.7 explicitly includes locally Lipschitz f in T(V,Y) and
identifies VDf with the tangential approximate derivative.
For stationary integral V one may set p=m and psi=0; q=2 is permitted
when m>2 and gives the strong critical exponent 2m/(m-2), provided
the other displayed mass, density, and interior assumptions hold.

The values in Upsilon are selected, not prescribed in advance.
The cited conclusion is unweighted and has no explicit Gaussian
witness/barrier coefficient. This confirms the prior-art statement,
not an automatic implication for the whole AE formulation.
