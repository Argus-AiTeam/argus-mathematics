The inherited setting is stationary and integral in an interior normal
cylinder, separated from its artificial vertical boundary over compact
base subsets. The section uses scaled height z, while the factors in
Lambda and gamma retain their physical units.

The source specifies uniform constants in the contact and bottom scale.

Multiplier convention, ex:moment and ex:multiplier-bounds:
    Gamma_s(z) = s^2 chi(Gamma_0(z)/s^2),
    U(s) = integral G_s(y-x) Gamma_s(z) dmu(y,z),
    |V_b|^2 <= Gamma_s,  |DV_b| <= C,
    |D Gamma_s|^2 <= C Gamma_s,  |D^2 Gamma_s| <= C.
The fixed smooth chi equals its argument near zero, is constant for large
arguments, and is comparable to min(t,1). V_b vanishes outside its
quadratic neighborhoods and at distances comparable to s.

The numerical comparison ex:comparison is

Here Z >= 0 on the contact path, alpha is positive for the stated small
k, and ex:barrier states that k is positive and bounded above by a small
fixed constant. Consequently the printed comparison gives
    U <= s^2 exp(-a)/k.
