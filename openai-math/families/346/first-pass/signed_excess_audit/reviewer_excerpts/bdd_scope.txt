Primary source inspected:
https://arxiv.org/html/2503.00651v1
Brena, Decio, De Lellis, Remarks and Conjectures on Stationary Varifolds.
Mathematical transcription of the inspected statements follows.

Throughout the relevant statements V is an integral m-varifold stationary
in the indicated cylinder; E_r is its normalized cylindrical tilt excess.

Theorem 1.9 (Height bound):
V is stationary in C_1, r in (0,1), E_1 is sufficiently small depending
on r,m,n,Q, and ||V||(C_1)/omega_m <= Q+1/2. There are Q points
y_h in the normal plane, not necessarily distinct, such that
    spt(V) intersect C_r
      subset union_h [pi_0 x closed B_{C(r)|log E_1|^{1-1/m}E_1^{1/m}}(y_h)].
If additionally Theta(0)=Q, the displayed stronger conclusion (1.10) is
containment in one strip of radius C_0 E_1^{1/2} about height zero.

Lemma 2.3:
0 < r_1 < r_2 < 1, eta in (0,1/2), E_1 is sufficiently small depending
on these parameters and m,n,Q, and ||V||(C_1)/omega_m <= Q+3/4.
There exists Q' in {0,...,Q} such that every C_r(x) subset C_{r_2}
with r > r_1 satisfies
    ||V||(C_r(x))/(omega_m r^m) in (Q'-eta,Q'+eta).
This is a definite-scale cylindrical mass conclusion.

Section 1.4 explicitly introduces "higher integrability" by computing,
for p>2, the expression in (1.32)
    [R^{-2} integral_{B_R minus B_{R_beta}} |Df|^p]^{1/p}
for the E_R^beta-Lipschitz two-valued catenoid approximation.
Equation (1.33) gives its asymptotics. The text concludes that this
quantity "grows faster than E_R^{1/2}, as beta < 1/2."
The integrand is a power of the gradient of the approximation, not a
power of distance in height from a finite configuration.
