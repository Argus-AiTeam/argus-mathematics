This is the official secondary summary of Vistoli, not the original PDF.

"This paper develops the foundations for intersection theory with
rational coefficients for Deligne-Mumford stacks."

For a separated Deligne-Mumford stack the paragraph states:
"There is a flat pullback, a proper push-forward and a generalized Gysin
homomorphism for regular local embeddings."

For its moduli-space morphism it states an induced rational Chow
pushforward "which is an isomorphism."

The excerpt supports that formalism, not an explicitly quoted stabilizer
formula. The old proof derives the coefficient
[k(A_c):k(B_c)] |I_B|/|I_A| using [A] -> [A_c]/|I_A|, and reduces its
projection formula to the descended multiple O_W(m) on the coarse space.
Those are geometric deductions to be read as part of the proof.
The original Vistoli PDF was not obtained/read by the old work or this
review. Do not relabel this paragraph as direct primary-paper verification.
