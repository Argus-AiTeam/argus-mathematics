Source: Andrei Mustata and Magdalena Anca Mustata,
Intermediate Moduli Spaces of Stable Maps.
Version: arxiv:math/0409569v4.
URLs opened: https://arxiv.org/html/math/0409569v4
and, by the separate source reader, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0409569v4 .
Locations: Definition 1.2 and Proposition 1.3 (PDF pages 5-6);
proof of Lemma 3.3 (PDF pages 17-18).

Definition 1.2 takes 0<k<=d, a flat genus-zero nodal family, a marking in
the smooth locus, a degree-d line bundle and its n+1 sections. Along with
the k-stability and basepoint-length restrictions it requires
"dim G_(p_1(s))=0 for any s in S".
Thus no basepoint is allowed at the marking. The graph-space definition
instead allows 0<=k<=d.

Proposition 1.3 gives natural transformations
Mbar_(0,1)(P^n,d,k+1) -> Mbar_(0,1)(P^n,d,k), for 0<k<d.
The pointed endpoint is k=1, not the graph-space endpoint k=0.

In the proof of Lemma 3.3:
"By a classical argument, pullback of the class [E] from this weighted
blow-up to Mbar_I^1 is -psi."
The same proof describes the graded normal bundle and says C^* acts
"with weights (1,...,d-l_I)".
The introduction also explicitly identifies O(1) of the first weighted
projective fibration with the cotangent class psi.

Notation and line wrapping are normalized to ASCII. This is prior
projective-space contraction/cotangent technology, not a proposition
asserting the arbitrary-target descendant lower bound.
