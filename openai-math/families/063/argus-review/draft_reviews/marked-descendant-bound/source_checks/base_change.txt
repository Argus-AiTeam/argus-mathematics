Displayed numbering on 2026-11-07: Lemma 36.30.4(1).

"Let S be a scheme. Let f:X -> S be a proper morphism of finite
presentation. Let E in D(O_X) be perfect and f flat. Then Rf_* E is a
perfect object of D(O_S) and its formation commutes with arbitrary base
change."

Notation and line wrapping have been normalized to ASCII. This is the
scheme statement. In the old proof it is applied on scheme charts to
the proper flat universal nodal curve and line bundles. The additional
fiberwise H^1 vanishing, constant h^0, vector-bundle conclusion and descent
across the stack charts are applications, not extra quoted conclusions.
