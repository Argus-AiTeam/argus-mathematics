Lemma 7.15 .
Let $i\geq 0$ and let $\mathcal{X}:=\mathopen{\{}i<\tau_{\mathscr{B}}\wedge\tau_{\mathscr{B}^{\prime}}\mathclose{\}}$ .
Suppose that $(\mathcal{A},I)$ is a template with $\lvert V_{\mathcal{A}}\rvert\leq 1/\varepsilon^{4}$ and let $\psi\colon I\hookrightarrow V_{\mathcal{H}}$ .
Then, the following holds.
(i)
If $\hat{\varphi}_{\mathcal{B},I}\geq 1$ for all $(\mathcal{B},I)\subseteq(\mathcal{A},I)$ , then $\Phi_{\mathcal{A},\psi}\leq_{\mathcal{X}}(1+\log n)^{\alpha_{\mathcal{A},I}}\hat{\varphi}_{\mathcal{A},I}$ ;
(ii)
If $\hat{\varphi}_{\mathcal{A},J}\leq 1$ for all $I\subseteq J\subseteq V_{\mathcal{A}}$ , then $\Phi_{\mathcal{A},\psi}\leq_{\mathcal{X}}(1+\log n)^{\alpha_{\mathcal{A},I}}$ .
