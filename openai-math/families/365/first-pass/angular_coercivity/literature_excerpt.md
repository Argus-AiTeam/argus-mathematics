# Scalar calculus used in the verification theorem

Source: M. S. Joshi, *Introduction to Pseudo-Differential Operators*,
arXiv:math/9906155v1 (1999).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9906155v1

The separate `angular-literature` research worker obtained and read the
versioned PDF. These excerpts were returned from that reading, not from
search summaries.

Theorem 5.5, Section 5, page 17, assumes properly supported classical
operators of orders \(m,m'\), and states:

> then \(PQ\) is also classical

with
\(\sigma_{m+m'}(PQ)=\sigma_m(P)\sigma_{m'}(Q)\).
Theorem 5.4 identifies the principal-symbol kernel with the class one
order lower. Together these imply the scalar commutator order reduction.

Theorem 6.3, Section 6, page 20:

> If \(P\in\Psi DO_{\rm cl}^m(\mathbb R^n)\) and is properly supported then
> \(P:H_c^s\to H_c^{s-m}\).

Section 6 proves continuity; Section 11, pages 44--45, applies localization
on compact manifolds. These are fixed-operator calculus results.
Uniformity in the finite symbol seminorms, the weight bookkeeping, and
the base-order cancellation are the responsibility of the proof in
`theorem.md`, not a claim that the source proves the campaign theorem.
