# Proposed removal of the ambient Kahler hypothesis

**Assessment: supported conditionally by the same analytic argument; novelty
is unverified.** This is CP_COMPLEX_EXTENSION version 1, not a consequence
of the finite checker or an extension of the entire branch theorem.

## Exact strengthening

Replace compact Kahler Y in the original curve bound by a normal irreducible
compact **complex analytic** fourfold with ordinary terminal singularities.
Retain Q-Cartier canonical sheaf and every specified resolution condition:
g globally projective, smooth source, isomorphism over Yreg, SNC exceptional
divisor, and principalized singular ideal with full inverse-image support.
Retain the connected smooth finite-punctured curve T0 and the stated
local-system conditions. The assertions are b<=N_T and the all-place
globalization bijection on pi1-surjective cores, with ordinary log
discrepancy and global model-prime conventions unchanged.

A07 states this in its opening scope and Theorem A, and closes Section 4
by inspecting the argument. Its claim of a strengthening is not a checked
novelty claim. The October 7 reference already contains the compact Kahler
bound; no claim is made that the non-Kahler formulation was absent from
all earlier literature.

## Audit of the hypothesis use

The projective analytic decomposition theorem used locally in the
[quotient argument](../link-map/report.md) does not require the global
source or target to be Kahler. A smooth local target embedding is enough
for its projective application. It gives the needed full-support summand
and canonical degree -2 quotient without global Hodge purity.

Compact curves are projective independently of whether their ambient
fourfold is Kahler. The specified globally relatively ample bundle on g
therefore supplies the explicit proper projective-family algebraization.
Whole-fiber GAGA, countable Quot loci and the finite-orbit argument use
that projectivity, not a global ample line bundle on Y. Their rationality
input is local klt analytic rationality.

The loop compact, integral extensions and line bundles use curve topology,
Stein theory, properness, coherent vanishing and the exponential sequence.
Fujino's theorem is for projective morphisms over a Stein base and a
full condition-(P) compact; it does not demand that the original compact
Y be Kahler. The local small model remains projective where needed.
The normalized curve families and degree-kernel argument likewise use
projectivity of fibers and the actual line bundles.

Finally the intrinsic canonical-comparison equation, local smooth
discrepancy bound, principalized full singular support, and connected
finite-cover argument for global primes have no Kahler hypothesis.
Compactness still provides proper global divisor families and finite
exceptional data. Standard terminal codimension-two smoothness is an
analytic local input, with the Appendix A reconstruction limitation
unchanged.

Thus the proof's dependencies do not introduce a hidden global Kahler
assumption at the curve-bound or bijection step. This is a conditional
proof reading of the proposed strengthening, not an existence construction
of a new non-Kahler example and not an independent kernel certification.
Removing global projectivity of g, or its principalization/SNC conditions,
would be a different claim and is not supported by this audit.

## Boundary at the branch-topology step

The October 7 `05-branch-topology.tex:281-321` expressly uses a compact
Kahler resolution for purity. The separate estimate
beta(Q)-sum c_2(Q_j^nu)<=sum b_T is not part of this extension's proof.
Its Hodge-theoretic hypotheses cannot be dropped merely because the
curve estimate is more general. Neither nonnegative junction difficulty
nor termination has been extended to arbitrary compact complex spaces.

Primary readers checked the exact analytic scope of Saito, Forstneric
and Fujino, and the explicit GAGA/Quot usage; see the linked unit reports.
Their source-scope support alone would have been only partial evidence.
The hypothesis-by-hypothesis argument above additionally checks the
remaining geometric uses. The same explicit standard inputs and
appendix slicing obligation remain as for the original bound.
No new mathematical counterexample search or unchecked novelty assertion
is part of this assessment. Native Reviewer acceptance is pending.
