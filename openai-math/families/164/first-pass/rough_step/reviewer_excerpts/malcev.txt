Source: Green and Tao, arXiv:0709.3562v4, Definition 2.1, its Remarks,
and Proposition A.10. Read in the retained primary-source HTML.

Definition 2.1 requires a nested ideal basis, adaptation to the
filtration, unique second-kind exponential coordinates, and that Gamma
consist exactly of elements with integral second-kind coordinates.

Exact statement from the Remarks:
"More generally there is a Mal'cev basis adapted to any filtration
G_bullet which consists of rational subgroups (cf. Definition 1.10)."

Quoted Proposition A.10, with mathematical typography normalized:
"Suppose that X = {X_1,...,X_m} is a Q-rational Mal'cev basis for
G/Gamma adapted to a filtration G_bullet. Suppose that G' subset G
is a Q-rational subgroup of G, and furthermore that G'_bullet is
a filtration on G' in which each of the groups G'_i is Q-rational
(with respect to the basis X). Write Gamma' := Gamma intersect G'.
Then G'/Gamma' has a Mal'cev basis X' = {X'_1,...,X'_{m'}} adapted
to G'_bullet in which each X'_i is a Q^{O(1)}-rational combination
of the X_i."
