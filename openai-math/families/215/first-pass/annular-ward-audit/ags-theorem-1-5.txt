"Fix any inverse temperature beta>0 and any scale k>=1. There exists
a one-parameter family of translation-invariant and strongly mixing
sub-graphs (G_m)_(m>0) of Z^2 on the same probability space such that
for m<m' we have G_m superset G_m', union_(m>0) G_m = Z^2 and

i) The XY-model on G_m of inverse temperature beta exhibits
exponential decay. More precisely, there exists a K=K(m) such that
for all x,y in G_m

E^(XY)_(beta,G_m)[theta(x).theta(y)] <= K exp(-m ||x-y||).

Furthermore, for any m sufficiently small:

ii) The set (G_m)^c is exponentially clustering.

iii) For any p>p_c(Z^2), i.i.d. edge percolation of intensity p
strongly percolates in G_m when m is sufficiently small (in the
sense that its complement has exponentially decaying tails)."
