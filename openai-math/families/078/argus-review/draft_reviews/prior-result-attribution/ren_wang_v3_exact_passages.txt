Ren and Wang, Furstenberg sets estimate in the plane.
Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2308.08819v3
Locator: Definition 3.1, HTML S3.Thmprop1.

"Fix delta in 2^(-N), s in [0,1], C>0, M in N. We say that a
pair (P,T) subset D_delta x T^delta is a delta-configuration if
every p in P is associated with a subset T(p) subset T such that
T intersects p nontrivially for all T in T(p) (note that T(p) does
not need to be all tubes in T passing through p). We say that
(P,T) is a (delta,s,C,M)-nice configuration if for every p in P
there exists a (delta,s,C)-set T(p) subset T with |T(p)| comparable
to M and such that T intersects p nontrivially for all T in T(p)."

Locator: Theorem 4.1, HTML S4.Thmprop1.

"For every epsilon>0, there exists eta=eta(epsilon,s,t)>0 such
that the following holds for any delta<delta_0(s,t,epsilon).
Let (P,T) be a (delta,s,delta^(-eta),M)-nice configuration with
s in (0,1], P is a (delta,t,delta^(-eta))-set, t in (0,2].
Then

|T|_delta >= c_epsilon
    delta^(-min{t,(s+t)/2,1}+epsilon) M."

The implicit positive constant c_epsilon is the source's
epsilon-dependent lower-comparison notation. Its exponent parameter
s is the tube-fiber dimension; t is the point dimension.
