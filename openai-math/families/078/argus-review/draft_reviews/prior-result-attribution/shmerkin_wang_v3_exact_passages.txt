"Definition 2.4.

Let $\mu\in\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{R}^{d})$, fix $\Theta\geq 1$, and let $\mathcal{A}$ be a finite partition of some set of full $\mu$-measure. We define the $\Theta$-robust entropy $H^{\Theta}(\mu,\mathcal{A})$ as

$\inf\{H(\nu,\mathcal{A}):\nu\in\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{R}^{d}),\nu\leq\Theta\mu\}.$

In the case $\mathcal{A}=\mathcal{D}_{m}(\mu)$, we write $H_{m}^{\Theta}(\mu)$ in place of $H^{\Theta}(\mu,\mathcal{D}_{m}(\mu))$."
