`build/sections/01-introduction.tex:9-31` defines, for Schwartz

`T_delta f(x)=integral (1-|xi|^2)_+^delta fhat(xi)

The theorem `thm:bochner-riesz` states:
for **every fixed `delta>0`** there is a finite `C_delta` such that

No bound uniform as `delta` tends to zero, and no order-zero
endpoint theorem, is asserted by that statement.
