                     2. L OCAL COMPLETE INTERSECTION MORPHISMS

   A morphism f : X → Y is called a lci morphism of codimension d if it factors into a
locally closed intersection X → P followed by a smooth morphism p : X → Y. Examples:
families of nodal curves over an arbitrary base; families of surfaces with mild singulari-
ties. Reason we care: often we want to consider families of things degenerating. We won’t
need this in the next two weeks, but it’s worth at least giving the definition.

  For any lci morphism f : X → Y of codimension d, and any morphism h : Y 0 → Y, we
have the fiber square
