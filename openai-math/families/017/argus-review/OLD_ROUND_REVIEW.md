## The two decisive nonformal arguments

**Center-independent density.** A rational `sigma` is chosen using the finitely many strict occupancy bounds before any centers. For each separated-basis ratio, the largest differing positive index contributes its newly chosen weight; common higher indices contribute only fixed powers of `theta`. Hence successive thresholds genuinely do not inspect center locations. A violating curve first lies in a constant nonzero-Y fiber. In each free-coordinate set `T`, use at most `nT` centers and the prechosen `|T|`-variable count. Persistence fixes another coordinate; inherited separation requires no new weights. The free set strictly decreases until a nonzero univariate polynomial would vanish on the affine-line curve, a contradiction. This is why coordinate collisions do not invalidate the extension's proof; the grid examples alone would not answer that question.

**Curve-to-jet.** The weighted monomial embedding identifies degree by a pure power attaining each maximal pole. A high-tail logarithm truncation preserves filtration and branch contact. On the ordinary blowup, the curve inequality gives nefness on noncontracted curves, while relative ampleness handles contracted curves and infinity misses the exceptional divisor. The displayed positive nef-plus-ample combination makes `A-E` ample even on a projective scheme. Noetherian degree-one Rees results give `p_*O(-nE)=I^n` and higher-direct-image vanishing **eventually**. Serre then supplies classes modulo `I^n`; since `I^n` is contained in the weight-at-least-`nR` ideal, those classes surject onto strict packets. High-degree section lifting supplies actual weighted polynomials without a small-degree normality assumption.

These arguments support the stated written claims subject to the explicitly recorded external inputs. No concrete gap was found within that scope. An unformalized step is not automatically a proof gap, but it must not be advertised as compiler verification.
