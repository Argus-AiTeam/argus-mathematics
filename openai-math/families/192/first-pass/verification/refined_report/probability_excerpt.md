## Theorem 3.4.1, p. 144

Let X_1, X_2, ... be i.i.d. with E X_i = mu and
var(X_i) = sigma^2 in (0,infinity). If S_n = X_1 + ... + X_n, then
(S_n - n mu)/(sigma sqrt(n)) converges in distribution to a standard normal.

## Theorem 3.10.1(i),(vi), pp. 196-197

The theorem states the equivalence of:

(i) E f(X_n) -> E f(X_infinity) for all bounded continuous f.

(vi) For all bounded functions f whose discontinuity set D_f satisfies
P(X_infinity in D_f)=0, E f(X_n) -> E f(X_infinity).

## Theorem 4.1.13, p. 212

If F_1 is contained in F_2, then
E(E(X|F_1)|F_2)=E(X|F_1) and
E(E(X|F_2)|F_1)=E(X|F_1).

## Theorem 4.1.15, p. 213

Suppose E X^2 < infinity. E(X|F) is the F-measurable variable Y
minimizing E(X-Y)^2.

## Theorem 4.6.2, p. 245

If phi>=0, phi(x)/x -> infinity as x -> infinity, and
E phi(|X_i|)<=C for all i in I, then {X_i:i in I} is uniformly integrable.
In particular phi(x)=x^p is allowed for p>1.
