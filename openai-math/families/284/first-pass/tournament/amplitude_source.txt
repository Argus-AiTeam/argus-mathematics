Equation (8), in the source notation:
Q^j |Psi> = sin((2j+1) theta_a) |Psi_1>/sqrt(a)
           + cos((2j+1) theta_a) |Psi_0>/sqrt(1-a).

Hypotheses: A is a measurement-free unitary preparation, the good subspace
is defined by a Boolean predicate, 0<a<1, and sin(theta_a)^2=a with
0<=theta_a<=pi/2. The following paragraph treats a=0 and a=1 separately.
Here Psi_1 and Psi_0 are the unnormalized orthogonal projections.

This supports the rotation identity, not by itself the manuscript's
fixed-trial cap or exact adaptive tournament guarantee.
