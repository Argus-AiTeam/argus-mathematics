Lemma 42.36.1:
Let (S,delta) be as in Situation 42.7.1, X locally of finite type
over S, and E finite locally free of rank r on X. For the associated
projective bundle (pi:P->X,O_P(1)) and alpha in CH_k(X),
pi_*(c1(O_P(1))^s cap pi^*alpha) is zero for s<r-1 and alpha for s=r-1.

Lemma 42.36.2 (Projective space bundle formula):
Under the same hypotheses, the map
direct_sum_(i=0)^(r-1) CH_(k+i)(X) -> CH_(k+r-1)(P),
(alpha_0,...,alpha_(r-1)) |->
pi^*alpha_0 + ... + c1(O_P(1))^(r-1) cap pi^*alpha_(r-1)
is an isomorphism.

https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02U1
Equation 42.37.1.1:
For X integral and E finite locally free of rank r,
sum_(i=0)^r (-1)^i c1(O_P(1))^i cap pi^*c_(r-i) = 0.
Together with 02TW this gives the pushforward recurrence.
Replacing E by U^vee and c_i(U^vee)=(-1)^i c_i(U) gives the
line-convention inverse-Chern series c_t(U)^(-1).

https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02UI
Lemma 42.40.3:
Let (S,delta) be as in Situation 42.7.1, and X a scheme locally of finite
type over S. If 0 -> E1 -> E -> E2 -> 0 is an exact sequence of finite
locally free sheaves, the total Chern classes satisfy
c(E)=c(E1)c(E2) in A^*(X).
This is the general formula. Tag 02UH only states the line-subbundle case.

Lemma 42.26.4:
Let (S,delta) be as in Situation 42.7.1; let X,Y be locally of finite
type over S, p:X->Y proper, alpha in Z_(k+1)(X), and L invertible on Y.

Definition 42.12.1:
For a proper morphism f:X->Y and an integral closed Z of dimension k,
f_*[Z]=0 if dim f(Z)<k, and deg(Z/f(Z))[f(Z)] if dim f(Z)=k.

https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02JU
Lemma 29.53.1:
Let f:X->S be a dominant locally finite type morphism of integral
schemes, with S locally Noetherian. For x in X and s=f(x),
dim O_(X,x) <= dim O_(S,s) + trdeg_(R(S)) R(X)
              - trdeg_(kappa(s)) kappa(x).
Equality holds if S is universally catenary.
For complex varieties this gives the usual fiber-dimension calculation.
