Theorem 6.8, excerpt:
"Let (V,(.|.)) be a simple strongly local unitary VOA. Then the map
I -> A_(V,(.|.))(I) defines an irreducible conformal net
A_(V,(.|.)) on S^1."

Reader's application check, not a quotation: the construction applies only
after the report's energy bounds and the corrected CTW primary-generator
criterion have established strong locality. It proves an irreducible
vacuum conformal net, not complete rationality, unitarization of arbitrary
modules, fusion positivity, or a categorical equivalence.
