Theorem 3.4, first sentence:
"Every positive-energy projective unitary representation pi of the
Virasoro algebra on a complex inner product space V integrates to a
unique strongly-continuous projective unitary positive-energy
representation U_pi of Diff^+(S^1) [universal cover] on the Hilbert space
completion H of V."

Proposition 3.2 gives, for a unitary positive-energy Virasoro
representation with c>=0,
||L_n v||<=sqrt(c/2)(|n|+1)^(3/2)||(L_0+1)v||.
Remark 3.3 gives the corresponding higher Sobolev estimates.

Reader's application check, not a quotation: these hypotheses hold on
the vacuum and on each already unitary ordinary module. No net is
assumed. Section 3.2 constructs the common smooth invariant core before
integrating the representation. For the particular Mobius transformations
used here, report.md additionally proves the growing-moment estimate and
the fixed-graph-norm spectral tail directly. These are analytic arguments,
not part of the finite Lean certificate.
