Locators: Proposition 3.17 and Theorem 4.5.

Proposition 3.17:
"Let V be a simple unitary vertex operator algebra. If V is C_2-cofinite
then ||a_0 q^(L_0)||<+infinity for all a in V and all q in (0,1).
In particular V is exponentially energy-bounded."

Theorem 4.5:
"Let V be simple unitary exponentially energy-bounded vertex operator
algebra and let U subset V be an energy-bounded unitary subalgebra of V
such that V is a finite direct sum of simple U-modules. Then V is
energy-bounded."

Reader's application check, not a quotation: the extension theorem assumes
an energy-bounded subalgebra and does not establish optimal energy order.
The report correctly distinguishes these prior results from its general
C_2-vacuum bootstrap. These two source statements do not by themselves
establish that the candidate generalization is new in the whole literature.
