Theorem 3, excerpt:
"Suppose that V is a vertex operator algebra which satisfies Condition C_2,
and let G be a finite group of automorphisms of V.
(i) For each triple (v,g,h) in V x P(G) and for each h-stable g-twisted
sector M, the trace function T_M(v,g,h,tau) is holomorphic in h."
Here h in the last phrase denotes the upper half-plane, not the group element.

Equation (8.6) identifies this function with
T_M(v)=tr_M(o(v) phi(h) q^(L(0)-c/24)).

Reader's application check, not a quotation: take the trivial group and
phi(1)=1. An ordinary simple module meets the sector hypothesis. For the
vacuum generalization, simplicity makes V itself such a module. Rationality
is added in part (ii), not part (i). The fixed factor q^(-c/24) does not
affect convergence of the positive graded trace used in report.md.
