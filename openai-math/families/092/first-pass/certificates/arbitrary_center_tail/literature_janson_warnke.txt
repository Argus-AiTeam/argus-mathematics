"each i in Gamma is included, independently, with probability p_i"
"Given a family (Q(alpha))_(alpha in X) of subsets of Gamma"
"I_alpha = 1_{Q(alpha) subseteq Gamma_p}"
"alpha ~ beta if Q(alpha) intersect Q(beta) != empty and alpha != beta"
"mu = E X = sum_(alpha in X) E I_alpha"
"Lambda = mu + sum_((alpha,beta) in X x X: alpha~beta) E I_alpha I_beta"
"P(X <= (1-epsilon) E X) <= exp{-phi(-epsilon) mu^2/Lambda}"
"phi(-1)=1"

The sum is explicitly indexed by ordered pairs. The family of supports
is not required to be injectively indexed. At epsilon=1 this is the
zero-event upper bound used for the literature comparison.
