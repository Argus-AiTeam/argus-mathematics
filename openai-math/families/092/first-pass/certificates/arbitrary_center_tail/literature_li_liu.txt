Source: Heng Li and Xizhi Liu, arXiv:2607.28429v2, Theorem 1.3.
Opened: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28429v2

"There is an absolute constant c>0 such that, for every sufficiently large
integer n, there is an origin-symmetric convex body K_n subseteq R^n such that
theta_L(K_n) >= c n log n."

The notation theta_L is defined in Section 1.1 as the infimum over
single full-rank lattices. It is not the unrestricted translative infimum.
