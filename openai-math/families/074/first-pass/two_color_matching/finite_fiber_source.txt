Saugata Basu, Algorithms in Real Algebraic Geometry: A Survey,
arXiv:1409.1534v1 (4 September 2014), Theorem 2.17.
Read at https://arxiv.org/html/1409.1534v1, Section 2.3.

"Let P,Q be finite families of polynomials such that the degrees of the
polynomials in P,Q are bounded by d, card P=s, and dim_R(V)=k', where
V=Zer(Q,R^k). Then,

  sum_{sigma in {0,1,-1}^P} b_0(Reali(sigma,V))
       <= O(1)^k s^{k'} d^k."

Notation in the same source, Section 1.1: R is a real closed field;
b_0 counts semi-algebraically connected components.

Application, not part of the quoted theorem: take R=the real numbers,
Q=the fixed-degree endpoint equations minus a target point, and
P={the endpoint Jacobian determinant}. Every point of the determinant
nonzero part of a fiber is isolated by the inverse function theorem.
The two nonzero sign conditions therefore bound the number of regular
preimages by a constant depending only on the fixed dimension and degree.
Positive-dimensional singular parts of the fiber are not counted.
