## Exact prior assertion and what the source supplies

`C_AT_PRIOR` v1 says that B_S(-p)<p^2/4 for 0<p<2 was already
stated in the primary literature by October 2026, so merely reproducing
that spectrum inequality does not establish a new old-Argus result.
Version 2 adds A_BIPS, not a stronger statement.

[Binder--Ivrii--Prause--Smirnov, arxiv:2610.23853v1, Section 1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23853v1#S1.SS4)
states B_b(t)<=t^2/4 on [-2,2], strictly on (-2,2) except zero.
Its following paragraph states
B(t)=max{B_b(t),3t-1} and the corresponding unrestricted strict range.
The actual quoted passage, including its proof-scope qualification, is
[`bips_excerpt.txt`](bips_excerpt.txt).

For t=-p in (-2,0), 3t-1<0<t^2/4, so the stated unrestricted
consequence gives exactly the old negative-parameter claim. Passing
between normalized B_S and the source's unnormalized B does not change
the exponents: multiplying a derivative or its integral mean by a fixed
positive constant adds a bounded term to the logarithm, whose ratio
to log(1/(1-r)) tends to zero.

This settles **stated prior scope**, not an independent proof of the
entire stronger theorem. Section 1.4 expressly supplies full details only
for the non-strict estimate and defers the perturbed-weight strict proof.
The original Makarov reference behind the displayed identity was not
independently retrieved. The local saved old excerpt quotes the bounded
theorem but not the following paragraph; its use for the unrestricted
claim needs the paragraph and normalization comparison supplied here.

The result is prior to the old Argus work, not evidence that the earlier
OpenAI manuscript contributions were not original. BIPS itself credits
the OpenAI preprints and their combination of mechanisms. Moreover,
a supremum-of-individual-spectra statement is not, without a separate
argument, the same statement as manuscript I's uniform constant and
exponent for all normalized maps. The old draft may retire a
spectrum-only novelty route without erasing that distinction.
