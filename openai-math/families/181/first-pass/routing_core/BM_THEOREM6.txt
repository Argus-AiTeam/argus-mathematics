Bucic and Montgomery, Towards the Erdos-Gallai Cycle Decomposition Conjecture,
arXiv:2211.07689v2, Theorem 6.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2211.07689v2#Thmtheorem6

Let r in N, and let H_1,...,H_r be a collection of hypergraphs with at
most ell vertices in each edge. Suppose that, for each I subset [r],
there is a matching in union_{i in I} H_i containing more than
ell(|I|-1) edges. Then, there is an injective function
f:[r] -> union_{i in [r]} E(H_i) such that f(i) in E(H_i) for each
i in [r] and {f(i): i in [r]} is a matching of r edges.

The mathematical symbols above are transcribed into ASCII. The
source's following Definition 7 explicitly permits repeated endpoint
pairs, treating the collection as a multiset.
