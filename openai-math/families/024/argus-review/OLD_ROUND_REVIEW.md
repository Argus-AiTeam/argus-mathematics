## Strongest existing analytic claim and evidence boundary

With all parameters formed from x, \(H\) fixed before \(x\to\infty\),
and \(K(t)\) the number of **ordered unequal good tuple pairs** of equal value,
the draft's strongest claim is
\[
 \frac{K(t)}{xG_m/\log x}
 \le C e^{-\kappa B/m^4}
       +C\sum_{h\ge H}h^4e^{-\kappa\rho^{-h}/h^3}
 \quad(0\le t\le x). \tag{T}
\]
Here \(B=\log\log x\), \(m\) is the manuscript's phase-dependent cut,
and \(C,\kappa,H_0\) are absolute. For each fixed \(H\ge H_0\) the estimate is
uniform in phase and all t once x is sufficiently large. The all-t statement
extends the original two-endpoint definition without recomputing its
parameters. The convergent tail tends to zero as \(H\to\infty\), and the first
term tends to zero for fixed H as \(x\to\infty\). This gives `prop:collisions`.

The external inputs are **Ford, `arxiv:1104.3264v2`, Lemma 5.1, pp. 21-24**,
and the **Mertens unit-box estimate in that version's proof of Lemma 3.1,
p. 11**. Their primary passages were opened, including the p. 21 statement
image to resolve extraction ambiguities. The application uses
\(k=b,\ell=1,f_1=1\), handles growing depth and \(b=1\), retains the smallness
restrictions and the proof's \(k\le\log_2 y\), and raises the last cutoff strictly
above z. Both residuals are decoded with repeated factors; values and witnesses
are not substituted for tuple pairs.

The projected mass follows from exact triangular simplex volume, the
elementary bound \(\rho^jg_j\le1\), bounded box enlargement and Mertens. It does
not use the main totient asymptotic or the recurrence asymptotic.
The Euler-product bound and needed prime-count upper bound are derived
elementarily. The separate, more general manuscript comparison in `layers.tex`
is not certified by declining to depend on it.

Compared with Ford's existing comparison theorem, the draft's claimed
contribution is this application-level uniform transfer and quantitative tail.
The present review does not extend it into new research. **Originality of the
historical mathematical claims remains unverified**, and no manuscript
refutation was established. Originality of the retrospective review itself is
not the requested outcome.
