# Primary-source reading of the endpoint distribution input

Bombieri, Friedlander and Iwaniec, *Primes in arithmetic progressions
to large moduli*, Acta Mathematica **156** (1986), 203-251.
Canonical identifier: `doi:10.1007/BF02399204`.

Primary PDF:
https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/117/6385-11511_2006_Article_BF02399204.pdf

The retrieved primary document is retained locally as `BFI1986.pdf`
for independent inspection, with printed p. 206 on PDF page 4 and
printed p. 204 on PDF page 2.

On printed p. 204 the notation is

    L = log x,
    psi(x;q,a) = sum_{n<=x, n=a (mod q)} Lambda(n).

On printed p. 206, equation (1.4) is

    sum_{q<=Q} max_{(a,q)=1} |psi(x;q,a)-x/phi(q)| << x L^(-A).

The sentence immediately following is:

> for any A>0 with Q=x^{1/2} L^{-B}, where B and the implied constant
> depend on A alone.

This is the ordinary endpoint Bombieri-Vinogradov theorem, not one of
the later large-modulus theorems with additional hypotheses. The sum
over positive moduli includes 1. Its statement is sufficient for the
weighted, shifted-endpoint argument in Section 2 of `verification.md`;
the weighted and shifted versions are proved there, not attributed
verbatim to this citation.
