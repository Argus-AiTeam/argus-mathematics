# Argus mathematics research program

## Public surfaces

The mathematics program has three complementary public surfaces:

| Surface | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Argus Open](https://open.argusbot.cn/) | Current open-problem registry, active targets, and highlighted results |
| [Full catalog](https://open.argusbot.cn/catalog) | Source-level audit of 757 historical problem records |
| This repository | Durable archive of completed result packages and verification materials |

The [broadcast page](https://open.argusbot.cn/broadcast) reports the current
research route. Individual `/workbench/<id>` pages expose read-only progress
for active targets.

## Current scale

The public portal and repository archive reported the following snapshot as of 2026-09-10:

- 757 historical source records under audit (617 reviewed, 593 independently verified);
- 102 confirmed-open problem cards on the public landing page (180 resolved and removed, 475 quarantined);
- 41 counterexample or proof campaigns across two execution waves;
- 44 archived public result packages in this repository (including Batch 001 and Tier-A/Tier-B campaign theorem packages; see [`docs/batch-001.md`](batch-001.md) and [`docs/publication-triage.md`](publication-triage.md)).

These numbers describe different layers and should not be added together.
Catalog records are source-audit units, active targets are selected research
campaigns, and result packages are completed public outputs.

## Subject coverage

The current catalog and workbenches span areas including:

- number theory and Diophantine problems;
- graph theory and combinatorics;
- knot theory and low-dimensional topology;
- smooth 4-manifolds;
- braid groups, representation theory, and algebra;
- algebraic and arithmetic geometry;
- dynamical systems and ergodic theory;
- complex and harmonic analysis;
- geometric group theory;
- metric geometry and mathematical physics.

The program is therefore broader than the single Hilbert's sixteenth problem
project shown on the main Argus website.

## From catalog row to published result

A typical result progresses through:

1. source and statement fidelity;
2. exact scope selection;
3. construction or proof development;
4. computational or formal replay where applicable;
5. adversarial review of the claim boundary;
6. public report, certificate, and reproducibility package;
7. external specialist review and correction when available.

The publication threshold is evidence-based. A promising route, partial
calculation, or model-generated proof sketch remains a workbench update until
the exact claim and its dependencies can be reviewed.

## Relationship to Hilbert16

The
[Hilbert16 Dual Argus Observatory](https://github.com/Argus-AiTeam/Hilbert16-Dual-Argus-Observatory)
is a separate, dedicated project within the broader mathematics program. It
tracks two isolated research processes investigating Hilbert's sixteenth
problem, Part II. Its existence should not be read as limiting Argus
mathematics to Hilbert16.
